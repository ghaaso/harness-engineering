## What a brief is
The whole instruction a session or helper receives for its role, written from the design's rules for that role. replace.mjs renders a task's brief (placeholders filled) into `in/brief.md` and hashes it into the manifest (`brief_sha`); the coordinator passes helper briefs (reader, checker, scorer) verbatim to the helper it starts, with a start message that carries only the variable lines named in the brief. Each brief states the task, the inputs, the output form and the rules, and never what result is hoped for. No brief names a model tier. No brief mentions seeds, plants, or the fact that anything is tested (the checker's brief never mentions seeding; the reviewer's never mentions planted assumptions).
Placeholders (the only ones allowed): `{{task}} {{epoch}} {{field}} {{folder}} {{role}} {{todo_json}} {{checkpoint_json}} {{must_read_json}}`. Used by: `child` all eight; `fresh-reviewer` all eight; `stpa`, `fmea`, `mapper` `{{task}} {{epoch}} {{role}} {{folder}}` (design-check tasks have no field); `reader`, `checker`, `scorer` none.
`{{role}}` shows the role as it is, except `control`, which replace.mjs renders as the neutral label `solo` (review U5-B-1), so the control arm's brief never names the comparison. No brief text may name the role `control` or say how the arms differ; the child brief says "If `in/reader-brief.md` is not in your folder, you have no readers" instead. The label covers the brief only: the manifest and the rendered settings' hook command still carry the real role (spec/replace.mjs.md step 9).
Last line: the `child` and `fresh-reviewer` briefs end with ``Your last line of every turn is exactly `TURN-END {{field}}` ...`` (R30); the design-check briefs end with ``TURN-END {{task}}`` because their `field` is null (CONTRACT §5 U5c; audit accepts it).

## Cannot be tested without the app
- Whether a real reader or checker obeys its brief when a page or item contains an instruction (the J4 fault); that a real reader returns exact quotes when the fetch tool returns a summary; that a scorer's guesses and a reviewer's findings are honest. These are measured by the pilot (the checker's J4 detection, the plants, the guess rate), not tested here.
- That the harness gives the helper only its brief and start message, and that the brief renders unchanged into `in/brief.md` (replace.mjs's tests).

## Round-3 skeleton (S0.5): the coordinator's brief
`tcb/briefs/coordinator.md` holds the acts (0) to (9) of one coordinator turn (FIX-JOIN part 2b) as
headings. Each place names the one fix that fills it, with "(empty until <fix>)"; an act that several
fixes touch has one sub-step per fix: 0a-0c (part 2b row 0) and 5a-5d (the fence hold in the order of
FIX-JOIN point 11, then the close of a session with the hook's steps of point 9). It has no agent file
and no placeholder: replace renders the briefs of task roles only.
