# Selections map: the solved design chosen for each problem of the research tree

This file is a reading aid. It restates the 103 mappings of the 2026-11-16 foundations research. Nothing here is new.
Source: `docs/program/evidence/research-tree/foundations-2026-11-16/map/result.json`. The last column is the line of the mapping's `"id"` in that file.
Columns: the problem is the record's `classical` text, shortened. Design and source are the record's `solutions` entries (names as written, source cut to author and year). The assumption column is the record's `fails_here`, shortened; where the record gives no remedy there, it says "remedy not recorded".
