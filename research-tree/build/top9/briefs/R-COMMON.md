# Readers: what the digest is for

Your digest feeds `research-tree/build/TRACK-DIGEST.md`, from which a plan is made for the owner's track: the
B-tree system (the research tree: built on git branch `build`, model-checked, never run for real; its pilot,
its merge, its open faults), graphs and knowledge graphs, embeddings and learned encoders, training on the 12 GB
card (24 GB later, not known yet), and how the quality mechanism (fewer mistakes, fewer hallucinations) is tested.
Token-saving work (the record book's store, the router, caching trials) belongs to a teammate: note it in one
line where it appears, do not digest it.

Write `research-tree/build/top9/out/<your name>.md` with these sections, each line with its place and mark:
1. What was built, run or measured, with its numbers (tokens, time, quality, hallucinations; say which measure).
2. Results that decide a choice: which method or form won, lost or was null, for which form and on which
   repository; what the record says must NOT be ruled out yet and why (one null is not a verdict).
3. Open faults, open questions, missing measurements, and what each needs (a fact, a measurement, an approve:).
4. Costs: tokens, time, memory, installs (with source and size if stated), and the basis of each figure.
5. Steps the record already proposes next for this track, with their stated cost and preconditions.
6. Not read (and anything you found named but missing).
Length: as long as the content needs, no padding; aim under 25 KB.
