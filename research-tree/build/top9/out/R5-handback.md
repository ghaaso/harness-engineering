## 2. Results that decide a choice

- Graph on top of embeddings is null to slightly negative in the one pre-registered study (-0.022 to +0.036 post hoc; hop study -0.004 to -0.007) (L35 :445, L15 :655). This is a result for the form tested; the hand-back keeps L15 and L44 as D2 arms, so a graph is not ruled out. [quoted]
- Embeddings (pretrained, small) beat grep by +0.058 to +0.101 Acc@10 at 0.6B-14B, 12 Python repositories (L35 :445); the 32B gain shrinks to +0.026. The graph must beat embeddings, not grep alone (:443). [quoted]
- A tuned retriever beats BM25 by large margins, but part of the 137M gain is its pretrained start (74.45 vs start 58.76, :1170); D3's strongest form needs 24 GB (:552, :558). Do not rule out until D2 Phase A and D3 run.
- Parser-built graph beats model-built on cost (2.13-2.25x vs 19.75-45.64x) and, in one suite, accuracy (L58 :865). Direction stands (:867). Ours already so; no test.
- Learned graph rankers: ULTRA and GREPO figures are on other tasks; seven temporal GNNs under GREPO (:1179). Untrained history forms (decay, TARMAQ, GitCF) and tuned fusion are the cheaper first tests, all inside D4 (:994-1009). Rule learners reach embedding level at 100-1000 s vs hours (L68 :1015).
- KG embedding training form moves MRR by up to about 10 points across studies; N3 gain 0.00-0.03 (L69 :1030). The earlier null (x09, E2) may rest on a weak training form; not sized by any source ("no source sizes the gain left over E2", :1030). One null is not a verdict: D4 with L69 is the retest.
- Tree and quality: the strongest independent evidence is for curated short instruction files (L2, +16.6 points, :10) and checking against an executed check (L11, silent failures 68-80 %, :40). Ranking rule put L2 first (:13). A reviewer reading several versions (L6) holds in part (:27). Self-correction without outside checks is not a gain (:1245 names Huang, Stechly as not read).
- Candidate selection with tests (L1): gains reported by five groups, each its own selector; oracle selectors overstate (:57).
- Hallucinations: project-symbol hallucinations are about 11 % of code hallucinations, at most 25 % (:130), so grounding to the symbol index addresses a minority; context gives more of the gain than the existence check (:1133).
- Model-written context hurts (-0.5 to -2, cost +20 %, :10); our briefs are model-written (:13).
