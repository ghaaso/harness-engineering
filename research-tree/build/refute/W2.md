## Small items

1. **The tests miss the central path** (rows 29 and 23). The coordinator's own order (call 1 in the background,
   call 2 at once, with the ensure registering during reconcile's wait) is untested. I recommend adding a test like
   `REFUTE byCall` (it takes about 7 s). Rows 11, 13, 25 and 17 also merit a test each: abandoned with a free slot,
   the `alarm_ms` bound, the hold-2 lock and ce check, and the `--arm-nonces` value check.
2. **Cited tests that do not show their departure:** departure 2 ("exit 0 without delay": no timing assertion),
   departure 3 (the FX-W1 test fails no hold after the slot write), departure 5 (the confirm test never runs
   reconcile with a new ce).
3. **Departures 4 and 5 together:** a corrupt `turn.json` restarts `turn_seq` at 1 and `unarmed_turns` at 0. So
   `turn_seq` is reset there, against departure 5. An abandoned ensure that saw a larger `turn_seq` then waits until
   `alarm_ms` (its `why: alarm_ms` exit wakes at an arbitrary time), and FX-W4's unarmed count restarts.
4. **Nonce files are never pruned.** `run/arm/<X>.json` grows by one per turn. Hold 2's prune removes only exit
   markers, and `exitMarkers`' directory read grows with the folder.
5. **Row 12's test** passes for `>=` under its own timing. Raising `turn_seq` only after a pause longer than one
   poll (for example 4.5 s) would pin `>`.
6. BUILD-PLAN lists lib `realProc` among W2's writes. Its `timeoutMs` argument was already in lib (S0.5), and W2
   changes only `spec/lib.md`. This is not a defect.
7. As the result says, the e2e never runs the real ensure (it uses a stand-in), and the merge conflicts with
   `u/PLAN` in `CONTRACT.md` and `spec/watch.mjs.md` remain for whichever unit lands second.
