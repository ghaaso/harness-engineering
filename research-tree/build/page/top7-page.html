Replays and second wave: results

Top session 7, 2026-11-23, 16:46 to about 18:57 UTC. Tokens are counted, not money. The replays are measured on records on this PC. The 14 keys are readings of papers; no experiment was run on them, and no figure of a lead was measured here.

1. The answer

  • Quality (the tree): none of the three cheap checks is worth piloting in the form tried. Gate on "done" with a check: it would have caught 1 of 92 contradicted claims. Ruff and mypy after edits: 0 of 10 located defects. A reviewer reading two versions: 2 of 17 overstatements caught, against 4 of 17 with one version. What caught the recorded faults was a second party with the code in hand or a stronger check.
  • Tokens, router: cache lapses rewrote 151.5 M of 927.4 M fresh tokens (16.3 %). Helpers waiting 5 to 10 minutes are the largest block, 46.2 M. The fixed start-up text is 19.8 % of fresh tokens (not 12.9 %: two scans missed 3,048 workflow helpers).
  • Tokens, graph: dropping stale reads loses on the meter in every project (net -93 M at 1.25x). Re-fetch is under 0.01 % of context. L5 and L4 are not piloted.
  • Tokens, library: 0 returns in 57 judged pairs (95 % bound about 100 of 949 helpers, 10.5 %). A null for one form.
  • The 14 keys: all four parts ended DONE. Graph grounding against hallucination first: a project's dependency context plus an existence check on names cut hallucination 73.56 % relative (MARIN, a maker's own study, small models, no agent); pinned-version API documents gained 10 to 20 points exact match in three groups' studies. Neither is tested here.
  • New: 17 leads (L59 to L75), 14 leads changed, 8 negatives (N16 to N23), one new test design (D4: the ranker redone with each family's best form).
  • Not found: no source shows a graph against no graph on code hallucination, and none measures an encoder on an agent's tokens.
  • Used: about 64.2 M tokens on the replays and judgment (cap 87 M); about 255 M on the wave (cap 600 M).
  • Mistake: three parts were started beside the replays; the app's limit of 20 helpers at once refused one part's reader starts. Cost about 12 minutes and 0.85 M tokens (section 2).
  • Asked of you: section 6, lines beginning "approve:".

  • 19.8 %, not 12.9 %.   • What is additive and what is not. L2's 5.5 points (prefix rewrites after a lapse or compaction) lie inside L24's lapses. In every project L2's part is below L24's lapse plus compaction plus break (harness 2.0 against 13.4 M, run 1.1 against 2.8, FixFlow 17.4 against 99.1, clinic 5.3 against 39.5). 703 lapses at 58 K is about 41 M, near the 37.6 M above.
