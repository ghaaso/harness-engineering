What was done

  • The plan was written from the track digest, the replays' judgement and the pilot plan, then checked blind and corrected.
  • Prices of the builder steps now come from measured roles, not judged ones; the check and the two card steps cost 3 to 10 times what the earlier plan said.
  • The pilot's hours fell from 8.5-13.8 to 4.2-9.2 once a 4.3 h PC restart was not counted as work.

Benefit and conclusion

  • The pilot is the only step that tests the tree's quality; its second comparison decides where review runs in every later run.
  • The stronger check tests the form that caught our recorded faults, on our own defects.
  • The two card screens can close or open learned rankers and text retrievers at our size, in these forms only; a null is not a verdict on the family.
