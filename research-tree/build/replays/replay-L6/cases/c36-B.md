## Result

All of the tests above passed on the machine and the date above (see [check-summary.md](check-summary.md) for the run). They prove structure and layout of synthetic data in one browser. They do not prove that a person can find the first exception on a phone, that a screen reader reads the tables sensibly, that the colours are readable in sunlight, or that the pages are fast on a real mobile connection. Those belong to S12's observed walkthrough with the people who will use them (O-14 asks whether the doctor reviews on a phone or a laptop).
