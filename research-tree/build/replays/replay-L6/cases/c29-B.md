## Needs the owner

### Entries between the physical count and the import's posting

The opening count is observed when the import batch posts, not when the count sheet was written. Anything that happened between the paper count and the posting (an issue at 08:30 when the import posts at 09:00) is refused as already in the opening, although the paper count did not see it. Either the import posts before the pharmacy opens after the count (a procedure, no code), or the count sheet's own time becomes the observation (an S05 and S10 change, not built). Which does the clinic want? Until then: post the stock import before any stock moves after the count.

### Who may pay opening pay

Opening pay is paid under the same rules as an accrual (`payroll.pay` with `cash.full`, from the salary account, never one's own). Q-09 (approved viewers and payers) still decides who that is.

# Opening cutoff guards: lock-removal experiments

Date: 5 November 2026, on this machine (Windows 11, PostgreSQL 17 in Docker, database `clinic_cutoff`). Code: branch `claude/epic-ardinghelli-d323a1` at `582ae4b`, with `test_cutoff_races.py` in the working tree.

Method: each experiment weakened one guard in one source file, ran one race from `test_cutoff_races.py` with real commits, and then restored the file byte for byte (checked by SHA-256). Nothing was committed with a guard removed. Runner: a scratch script outside the repository (`lock_removal.py`). It ran pytest with the check's settings (`clinic.settings.test`, plugin autoload off, `pytest_django.plugin`).

This is local evidence only. The outcomes depend on how the two threads interleaved on this machine.

| Experiment | What was changed | Race | Result | What it shows |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| baseline-blocked | Nothing removed. The test's `if seen.race.second_was_blocked:` was replaced by an assertion that it was. | All five | **Pass** | On this machine the second call waited in every race, so the lock-evidence assertions were checked, not skipped. |
| cash-check-before-lock | In `receive_cash`, the cutoff check was moved before `lock_accounts`, so it read without the account lock. | Receipt on the cutoff racing the cash opening | **Fails** | The receipt read no opening, then queued on the account row, and then reached the database. The journal-line trigger (`imports/0005`, which locks the account) refused it with an `IntegrityError` instead of a plain refusal, so nothing was counted twice. The service's account lock decides that the refusal is a plain one. The test detects the removal. |
| pay-check-before-lock | In `accruals._post_original`, the pay-opening check was moved before `lock_accounts` on payroll payable. | Accrual approval racing the pay opening | **Fails** | The same pattern as cash: the accrual trigger (`imports/0006`) refused with an `IntegrityError`. The payroll payable lock decides that the refusal is a plain one. The test detects the removal. |
| pay-opening-document-lock | `payments._lock_documents` (FOR KEY SHARE of the opening documents) was made a no-op. | Paying an opening obligation racing its reversal | **Fails**, on the lock evidence | The outcome assertions held: the reversal won and the payment was refused. Without the document lock, the payment queued on the account rows the reversal holds, not on the opening's document. The test detects the removal through the row it waited for. The outcome on this machine did not depend on the document lock. |
| stock-receipt-scope-lock | The receipt's `lock_scopes` before its cutoff check was removed. | Receipt racing the stock opening | **Passes** | Not load-bearing. S03's posting locks the medicine's item row first, and S10 holds that row from registering the opening's batches. The race test asserts that the receipt waited on the item row. The scope lock stays: it keeps the ledger's lock order, and it is the lock for the paths that do not take the item row (issues, returns, adjustments). |

## Conclusion

On this machine:

- The cash and pay service locks decide whether a racing posting gets a plain refusal. Without them, the database triggers still refuse, but the person would see a server error. Either way nothing is counted twice.
- The opening-document lock decides where a payment waits, not the outcome.
- The stock race is serialized by the medicine's item row before the scope lock is reached.
- No experiment let a guarded posting through.

Not tested here:

- Issues, returns and adjustments racing an opening, which rely on the scope lock alone.
- Writes made around the services racing an opening. The pay and stock database checks take no lock, as recorded in the task record.
