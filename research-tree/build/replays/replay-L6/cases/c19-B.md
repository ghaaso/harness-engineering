## Needs the owner

### The stock count sheet states the end of the cutoff day

Since root gate round 1 (GC-01) a stock opening is the shelf at the end of its cutoff day, as cash and pay openings are, and the stock import posts only after that day. A paper count taken during the day must therefore be brought to the end of the day: whatever was delivered, issued or removed after the count that day is added or taken off on the sheet, and is never entered in the application afterwards. What happens the next day is entered once the import has posted, with its own time. Can the clinic count at closing, or bring a daytime count to the end of the day? If not, a count-time observation would need an S05 and S10 change (not built).

### Who may pay opening pay

Opening pay is paid under the same rules as an accrual (`payroll.pay` with `cash.full`, from the salary account, never one's own). Q-09 (approved viewers and payers) still decides who that is.
