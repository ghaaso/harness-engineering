### S05 bill-line interface

PROPOSED for the reconciliation slice; nothing in S04 depends on it yet.

- Read `clinic.issuing.references.unlinked_period_lines()`: live ordinary `BillLine` rows (their bill's document not reversed) with no live link to an issue. In count-derived mode these are the period lines; in full-capture mode a line appears here only until its issue links to it.
- Read `bills_in(start, end)` for bills by the date written on them, and `alive_lines().filter(bill__bill_type="missing_medicine")` for the lines a case may link (never period lines, never linked to issues).
- Each `BillLine` has `bill.bill_date` (the date written, not the date entered), `item`, `quantity` in base units, `entered_quantity` with `conversion`, and `document` (the `BIL` or `ISS` that wrote it).
- S05 adds the period assignment as its own append-only table referencing `BillLine` (the reviewer assigns boundary lines dated on a count day; BILL_LINE_ASSIGNED once assigned), and must decide what reversing a bill entry with assigned lines does: S04 has no knowledge of assignments, so either S05 registers a document dependency from its assignment document to the bill document (S02's `depends_on`, which then refuses the reversal with DEPENDENT_POSTINGS) or S05's view treats a reversed line as void.
- The issue-side counterpart for full capture is the issue's movements (`StockMovement` of the `ISS` document, kind `issue`) and `IssueBillLink`.
