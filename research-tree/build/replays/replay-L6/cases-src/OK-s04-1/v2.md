## Lock-removal experiments

Each race names the lock it depends on. An experiment replaces one call, in `clinic.issuing.issues` or `clinic.issuing.references` only (the ledger's and kernel's own locks stay), and runs the same race. The experiments are tests in `test_issue_races.py` and `test_reference_races.py`, so they run in every check. Since review round 1, a race either holds the first writer after its whole body (`held`, S02's `hold_then_race`) or pauses it at a named checkpoint before its deferred foreign-key checks (`paused`, `issuing.testing.pause_then_race`). Those checks take FOR KEY SHARE on referenced rows, and in the `held` timing they had made the item lock look like a second guard of FEFO (D04-23).

A probe run while fixing finding 3 also showed why the item lock changed from FOR UPDATE to FOR NO KEY UPDATE. With the other writer paused while holding its scope, the issue held the item FOR UPDATE and waited for the scope. The writer's deferred checks then needed FOR KEY SHARE on the item, and the two deadlocked. The kernel retried, and in that run the other writer's posting never committed (review round 1, finding 2). The probe was a throwaway test and is not in the suite. The paused FEFO race (`test_fefo_waits_for_a_writer_paused_after_its_movements`) now passes with both postings committed.

Every race also records `second_was_blocked` where waiting is expected. That is timing evidence on this machine only; the asserted outcomes do not depend on it.

## Not verified

- The screens with a person, a phone, a screen reader or a receipt printer; the print failure is simulated by failing the page's render on the server.
- A closed period on an S04 posting (S02's period guards refuse it; no S04 test closes a period).
- The inactive-item refusal through S04 (no service deactivates an item).
- The count gate is exercised by setting the scope's gate directly, since counts are S05's.
