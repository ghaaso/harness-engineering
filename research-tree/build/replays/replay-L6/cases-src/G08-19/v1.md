## Proposed changes to shared documents (the root applies them or records them as open)

1. **Contracts, capabilities**: `payroll.read` to see any pay record; `payroll.write` to prepare and correct; `payroll.approve` to approve and to reverse an accrual or correction; `payroll.pay` with `cash.full` to record and reverse a pay payment. Seeing a document that moved a salary-paying account needs `cash.full` as well as its class (D08-14). Nobody prepares, approves, pays or reverses a document of their own linked pay record (D08-08, D08-20).
2. **Contracts, routes**: `/pay/`, `/pay/approvals/` (and `?accrual=`), `/pay/staff/`, `/pay/accruals/new/`, `/pay/accruals/{id}/`, `/pay/payments/new/` (and `?accrual=`). Every pay address answers one identical FORBIDDEN without `payroll.read`.
3. **Data model**: rows for `Employee`, `PayPeriod`, `PayAccrual` (versioned draft document: `corrects`, `previous`, `version_no`, policy version), `PayComponent`, `PayPayment`, `PayPaymentLine` as built (D08-01); settlement kind `pay_reduction` (D08-09).
4. **S02 conventions**: the two additive hooks (`HIDDEN_RULES` in visibility, `REVERSAL_CHECKS` in reversal) as the way a module adds a view rule or a reversal rule without editing the kernel. Note for later slices: Django's foreign keys are checked at commit, so `Posting.depends_on` does not lock the referenced document during the command; a command that must wait for a reversal in flight locks the document row itself (D08-19).
5. **Decision log**: D08-02 to D08-13 as provisional answers under Q-09, and D08-08 and D08-20 under Q-03.
6. **Glossary**: rows for the PROPOSED pay pages (staff, prepare, accrual record, payment); UI-58 R4 is one combined table of corrections and payments, the payment rows shown only to `cash.full` holders; UI-59 has no R3.
7. **S07 gate W5**: closed by D08-14; the root can mark it done.

## Needs the owner

Under Q-03:

- Who holds `payroll.read`, `payroll.write`, `payroll.approve`, `payroll.pay` and `cash.full`, and whether every `cash.full` holder should also hold `payroll.read`.
- Whether one person may both approve an accrual and record its payment. Today only maker and approver are separated.
- Whether one `payroll.approve` holder may reverse an approved reduction or accrual alone, or whether a second person must confirm.
- Whether a `payroll.read` holder without `cash.full` should see the paid total of each accrual. Today the total is shown and the single payments are not.

Under Q-09:

- Period length, and which kinds of pay and components exist (today one type, `regular`, and manual lines).
- Whether a reversed accrual may be accrued again for the same period. Today the period stays taken and only a correction follows (D08-07).
- How an overpayment, an advance or a recovery is handled. Today all are refused.
- Due dates, the pay policy that replaces `S08-PROPOSED-2026-11-04`, and who may view pay.

## Known limits (recorded, not fixed)

- Guard coverage: nothing in the database ties a `PayPaymentLine` to the ledger allocation it describes, or `PayPayment.employee` to the settlement's counterparty. Migration `0007` does not compare the correction's journal amount with its difference. The service writes both together; see [review round 1](evidence/review-round-1.md), finding 5.
- A payment and a reversal of its accrual can deadlock (40P01), and `run_command` retries the loser (finding 6).
- The races show behaviour on one Windows machine with a local container, not under production load or for interleavings not tried.
