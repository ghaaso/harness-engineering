## Opening cutoff guards

S10 refuses an opening once anything is recorded for the medicine, supplier, account or employee. Since 5 November 2026 (D10-40, D10-38's note) the other modules also refuse what is recorded later with a date on or before the cutoff of a standing opening: S07 cash receipts, payments, transfers, corrections and closes (a cash opening is the balance at the end of the cutoff day); S08 accruals, corrections and payments (a pay opening is the unpaid pay at the end of that day); S04 issues, S03 receipts and supplier returns, and S05 adjustments and count applications whose event is before the opening count's observation or dated before its day (a pay period may not run across a pay opening's cutoff either); and S06 posts an import's supplier opening draft only through the batch's own posting. An opening pay obligation can be paid: S08's payment allocates to it in full, never more than is due. Still not guarded: (1) the pay payment screen (UI-58) does not list opening pay balances, so staff cannot yet pay one from a screen (S08's payment command can); (2) the database checks for pay and stock (`imports/0006`, `0007`) read committed rows without taking the lock, so a write made around the services while an opening is being posted is not held back by them (the services are serialized; the cash line check locks the account); (3) for stock, nothing that happened between the physical count and the import's posting can be entered, because the opening count is observed when the batch posts (owner question in the task record). Q-11 still blocks any real file. The cutover page and every reconciliation sheet repeat this.
