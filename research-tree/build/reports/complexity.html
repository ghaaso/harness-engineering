His review idea

It stays measured. It is F1: the report writer’s same-window review against a fresh refuter’s review, both of the same planted copy, scored blind. Per field it adds 19.1 M tokens (11.6-34.9): the same-window review 9.9 M (6.3-17.8), the fresh review 7.4 M (4.2-12.4), the scorer 1.79 M (1.07-4.68). The planted copy is written once, 8.05 M (4.94-10.39). Together 142 M tokens (86-255) for seven fields. Without both arms reviewing the same copy, the scorer would have nothing common to score.

The same-window review runs only where the report ended under 296 K tokens; otherwise that field’s pair is recorded as not run. About five pairs if round 3’s spread holds (3 of 4 fields).

It measures same-window against fresh review, as paired results over those fields: planted mistakes found with the right reason (the main result), citation failures, use outside conditions, tokens, and the same-window review’s factual sentences that cite nothing.

It does not measure comparison 1 (tree or fan-out against one session); the spread between two fresh reviews (there is one fresh arm); or the scorer’s error (one scorer). Each pair is also a different field. The refuter reads our planted files before it refutes; a separate fresh reviewer would keep them out of its window for 2.8 M tokens more a field; not taken.

Readers and writers side by side

  • Readers at once: saves time, not tokens. Per field, 22-37 min at once against 48-110 min one at a time; the whole field 70-106 min against 118-181 helper-minutes.
  • Splitting the reading: saves tokens. Five readers of 31 calls cost 13.9 M tokens; one reader doing all 155 calls would cost 33.2 M and reach 369 K, past compaction.
  • Fields: seven at once 1.2-2.0 h in (e), 2.8-6.0 h in (e2) at three at once; one after another 13.7-21.1 h (median 19.0 h), under a day. Path (a) one node at a time: pilot 5-7 h, preparation 3 h, leaves 13.5-16.5 h, and its 75-140 typed turns spread it over days.
  • Several writers on one file: saves nothing in any research path. Every reader, extractor and report writer writes its own file, and the ledger and task table have one writer under the run lock. It applies only to the plan’s fix round, whose three units run one after another because two change the same settings templates; own copies landing in one order would take about 1.2 h, not 3. Paths (e) and (e2) have no fix round.
