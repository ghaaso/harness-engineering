Does the tree rely on the OS’s locks?

From LOCKS.md version 2, a read-only check of 16 places against eight papers (the CMU course, Graefe, SQLite, Chubby, mmap). Nothing was built or run. [e] marks an estimate; L1 to L16 are the places in the table below.

  1. No. Our locks are files or folders made by exclusive create. The run and watcher locks name their holder and each write re-checks a nonce, epoch or revision; the lane’s, lander’s and plan gate’s do not; the run’s gate takes none.
  2. What you saw is on this page; the pilot plan takes no lock. Two OS proposals are here, neither built: a lock the OS frees when its holder dies (a test first), and read-only and append-only rights on evidence, which is not a lock.
  3. The course’s cost reason (an OS mutex’s 25 ns) does not carry over to holds of seconds; its control reason does, and every wait here is bounded. Its lock table need not survive a crash; our lock files outlive their holders, so they need stale breaks, where most lock faults sit; a lock the OS frees removes those, as in SQLite.
  4. Flaws, none from an OS lock: the lane and plan gate can let two holders in; if two run-gate calls overlap, which the coordinator’s brief avoids, one 117.7 M field too many can start; shuffle.mjs rewrites its key on a repeat (small).
  5. Change before the research run: the gate writes each decision once to a numbered slot, and the key is made once. After the pilot: the run lock’s checked break for the lane and plan gate, then the OS lock if its test passes. All on Opus with a blind refuter; estimates: about 9-22 M tokens each (the key less), the OS lock about 43 M.

Counts, one verdict per place (16 places that take a lock, claim, fence, lease, slot or exclusive write, or append with none; not faults, not call sites): flaw 4 (L8, L10, L11, L12); sound 11 (L1-L7, L9, L13, L14, L16), sound for their data though L1, L2 and L16 keep a liveness gap; not known 1 (L15).

What would change, and when

Each replacement is invariant-sensitive code, so it runs on Opus with a blind Opus refuter. One such unit measured 42.9 M tokens (28.5-51.4). A small change is put at 0.2-0.5 of a unit, a guess on no record: 8.6-21.5 M. Every cost below is an estimate.

  • Before the research run, the gate (L11): each decision written once by exclusive create to the next numbered slot; a taken slot means another gate decided in between, so it reads and decides again. No lock is held, so a killed gate leaves nothing to break. The brief’s && rests on a model’s obedience, which the design does not rely on, so the code changes. A lock broken by pid alone was rejected: a dead gate whose pid is reused would hold every start. Cost 8.6-21.5 M [e].
  • Before the run, the key (L12): made once; a repeat finishes and never re-keys. A refusal whenever the key exists was rejected: a crash after the key would stop the field, since no helper may clear sealed/. Cost: smaller than the gate fix [e].
  • After the pilot, the plan gate (L10): the run lock’s checked break, judged by pid and start time, not age. Cost 8.6-21.5 M [e].
  • After the pilot, the lane (L8): the checked break, no wall-age rule, the slot published with its owner record in one step. Cost 8.6-21.5 M alone [e], or inside the 43 M unit below.
  • After the pilot and its test, the OS lock: a named pipe held by the holder; a second listen on the name is refused at once and the OS closes it at death. It would delete the PowerShell judgement, the stale breaks, and A11, D10 and D21. It does not replace the fences, and the next holder still cleans up what a dead holder half did. About 1 unit, 43 M (28.5-51.4) [e]. The test needs your word (CLAUDE.md rule 3).
  • Not proposed: OS file protection for evidence is an access right, not a lock, and no source speaks against it. An embedded database as lock manager (SQLite) is not indicated: the run lock and fences are sound, and it would rebuild the 4,501-line library, about 129-215 M [e].
