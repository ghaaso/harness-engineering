Brief: `briefs/GRAPH-TRANSFORMER-QUESTION.md` (the owner's words outrank this file). Read only: code under `improve/code/`,
`runbig/card/d4/d4lib.py`, outputs under `CR/improve/` (CR = `C:\Users\User\Documents\projects\card-run-2026-11`). Nothing

**Measured here (node, CPU seconds, tuning only).** Read `CR/improve/U4/data/<repo>/{plan.json, blocks/*.json}` and only the
lines of `commits.jsonl` up to the last tuning task (every held-out task comes later; the script checks it). No held-out
label, table or score was opened. Two scripts in the session scratchpad (`reach.js`, `hops.js`). Their pair-event counts
before tuning (3,865 / 5,994 / 342,722) equal U6's `n_train_events`, and their history reach of tuning truth (0.697 / 0.342 /
0.787) equals T1's 70 / 34 / 79 %, so they read the same data the runs read. Order in every triple: FixFlow / clinic / zulip.

**Answer.** The plateau is not explained by over-squashing or over-smoothing. It is explained by modes that are present:
node-wise embeddings that cannot hold pair facts (U5's graph alone 0.107 against counting's 0.124), a temporal neighbour window
that holds about half of the truth counting can reach (DyGFormer, TGN), a training leak across batches inside large commits
(pair-stream TGN only), and selection on the same 260 tuning commits (every good tuning number is optimistic). The one good
number that could be "too good" for a reason other than selection is T2's diff feature (row 16): not settled.
