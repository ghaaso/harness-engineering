Review of the running system

25 November. Four independent reviewers each attacked one part: card sharing, the graph models, the search models, and the path to the final numbers. Times are your local time.

Seven serious faults found. All confirmed against the code. None has reached a reported number.

7 repaired 1 cause still open

Would have made a result wrong

1Search-model training used an eighth of the planned data

Repaired

A helper cut the largest repository's training data to 2,500 past changes; the plan says 20,000. Nobody recorded it. The full data is rebuilt and training has restarted on it. It takes several times longer, which moves the finish to the weekend.

2Time-aware graph models judged almost untrained

Repaired

The selection step could only pick models after two rounds of training, because the repositories are scored at different points. The method would have looked worse than it is.

3Job scripts reported success when a job failed

Repaired

A failed step passed unnoticed, and a half-finished tuning run could have been frozen as the final choice.

Would have stopped the last steps

4A shared results table saved in the wrong format

Repaired

Since last night every later step that reads it would have failed, the final combining step included. The content was whole; only the format was wrong.

5Writing the graph results into the tables fails at once

Repaired

Both steps save a compressed file and read it back as plain text. Neither had ever run on real data. It would have shown only after all the final training.

Left the graphics card idle

6The large AI model held a third of the card for nothing

Repaired

It built lookup tables far larger than it uses. That is why it ran out of memory twice and waited for the whole card. Same results, much less memory.

7A job that needs the whole card could leave it empty

Repaired

When other programs such as Chrome used more than about 2.5 GB of the card, that job could not start, nothing else was let in, and nothing was logged. Other jobs now use the card meanwhile.

Why jobs hung: the graphics driver resets under load

Cause open

Windows logged four driver errors since the Studio driver went in on 24 Nov at about 18:38. The crashes of the whole PC stopped; the resets did not.

24 Nov 18:55A job was hit. Its results are whole. 24 Nov 20:02A job was hit. Its results are whole. 25 Nov 05:19The large AI model's run died and was rerun. 25 Nov 09:02A graph job hung. The card sat idle 4 h 20 min.
  • Hang check on every card job: a silent job on an idle card is stopped within about 20 minutes and restarted from its save.
  • Driver-error alarm: the moment Windows logs the error, the jobs on the card are stopped and restarted.
  • Not done: removing the cause. A driver or system setting is yours to approve.

Smaller gaps closed on the way

  • Work without an owner: the large AI model's remaining work had nobody assigned. It has now.
  • An alarm that could not go off: the coordinator's own alarm for card problems never fired. Rewritten.
  • After a reboot: nothing restarted by itself. One command now restarts everything from its saves.
  • Hang check too eager: it could have stopped one long, quiet, healthy job. Fixed.
  • Short jobs asking for the whole card: they now ask for what they use, so they can share.
  • A "known fault" that was not one: an order to change a correct step was withdrawn before it broke it.

Checked and found sound

  • No future data in training: every training example comes from before the cut-off, in all three repositories.
  • The sealed test answers stay sealed: no training, tuning or selection code can read them.
  • Like for like: each new method and its baseline are scored on the same questions, the same files and the same scorer.
  • Saves are whole or absent: no half-written saves were found. The two saves that failed on the full disk were redone.
  • Resumed runs match straight runs for the time-aware graph models.
  • The card was never overbooked since jobs began sharing it.
  • The new hang check stops the whole job, not just its outer shell.

Listed, not repaired

Real but unlikely to bite in what remains, or cheap to recover from. They are written down for after the round.
