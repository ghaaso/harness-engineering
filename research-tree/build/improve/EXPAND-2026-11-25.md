## 1. Each arm: what the 12 GB card or the clock forced, and where the room is

Code paths are under `improve/code/`.

**U1 (A7, A8, A3, A2; D2).** CPU work; the card set only the two dense features: untuned Qwen3-Embedding-0.6B and
SweRankEmbed-Small at fp16, 512 tokens, batch 32 (`U1/dense_u1.py:57,62`). LambdaMART grid 7/15/31 leaves x 3 learning
rates, up to 1,000 trees (`U1/ltr.py:23,26`). Results on 217 tuning queries: A2 0.919 Acc@10 (five-fold 0.916), BM25
0.713; per repository A2 is 0.912 / 0.865 / 0.979 and P@2 0.500 / 0.446 / 0.545 (`units/U3/report.md`). Where the room
is: not Acc@10 (zulip is at 0.98, the candidate rows hold 97-100 % of the truth files). The room is P@2 everywhere and
the clinic on both measures. The clinic has 37 tuning queries and 189 pre-cutoff candidates: that is a data limit, and a
bigger model does not add clinic history.

**U2 (A5; D2).** Shaped by the 12 GB card: training at 256 tokens while scoring at 512 (`U2/trainlib.py:11` and its
inference note at line 56); batch 8, so only 8 queries and 8 x (1 + negatives) chunks per contrastive step, not swept
(`trainlib.py:71`); Qwen3 by LoRA rank 16 with gradient checkpointing, not in full (`trainlib.py:9,23`); one seed. The
only number so far, SweRank stage 1 at 0.837 tuning Acc@10, came from the void 2,500-commit pairs. Room: as a feature
of A1, and on FixFlow and zulip, which have the pairs. On the clinic (about 189 pairs) more capacity cannot help.

**U3 (A4, A6; D2).** Most shaped by the card. Both 7B models at 4-bit nf4 (`U3/a4.py:84`, `U3/embedlib.py:125-128`).
A4's query cut to 1,024 tokens, "the card's memory does not allow 32k" (`a4.py:19`); the authors keep all of it and
take the top 100 (`run_rerank.sh`), ours 20 or 30 (`a4.py:22`). A6's chunks cut at 512 tokens and its batch lowered from
16,384 to 8,192 tokens to fit (`a6.py:19,21`). A4 on tuning: Acc@10 +0.014 (-0.002 to +0.039) and P@2 -0.022 (-0.071
to +0.028) against A2: level. On the clinic its P@2 is 0.351 against A2's 0.446. Reading: a 7B text-only reranker does
not beat a learned stack that also sees history. Whether 4-bit cost anything is unmeasured (CANDIDATES A4: "the 4-bit
loss is a measurement"). The record's bigger lever is the 32B reranker (f3a C01: 88.69), so this is where size can buy
something, and only on top-2. A6 has not run.

**U4 (B3, B2; D4).** CPU work. The clock, not the card, set zulip training at 100 of 1,044 blocks (U4 report, "not
done"). CONTENT_COS is Qwen3-0.6B file vectors at 512 tokens (`U4/embed.py:27`), one snapshot per segment, empty for
88-99 % of FixFlow and clinic training cells: a snapshot rule, not capacity. EASE sits at the top of its lambda grid,
so it collapses to a weighted count. B2: +0.032 FixFlow, +0.035 clinic, +0.006 zulip, +0.013 pooled. The pooled zulip
model is cut at 50 trees against 620-750 elsewhere: zulip's signal stops early. More zulip training blocks is the
only cheap untried lever there.

**U5 (B4; D4).** Not shaped by the card: under 2 GB (CANDIDATES B4). Grid: SAGE or GAT, 1-2 layers, hidden 64/128,
negatives 5/20 (`U5/train.py:214`). Its 22 runs span 0.1275 to 0.1314 pooled tuning P@10 (`CR/improve/U5/choice.json`);
the frozen sage_L1_H128_N20 at 0.1314 is below B2's 0.138 and above the untrained 0.124. Node features: Qwen3 0.1314,
none 0.1291, SweRank 0.1282. **Capacity and text are not the limit**: two layers or 128 units add nothing, and text adds
0.002. A bigger network or a 7B node embedder is unlikely to help; the limit is the signal in co-change.

**U6 (B5; D4).** DyGLib's defaults: DyGFormer channel 50, 2 layers, 2 heads, patch 1; TGN 1 layer (`U6/u6models.py:117-125`);
batch 200 (`U6/core.py:18`); neighbours 20/32; one seed. With the epoch chosen per repository (the repair): DyGFormer
0.1076 pooled, TGN 0.0393, both under the untrained 0.124 (`refute/IMPROVE-SYSTEM-graph.md` B1). The record already says
counting beats these models on some streams (f3c C23, C71-C75, as CANDIDATES B5 cites). Longer neighbour lists fit a
96 GB card, but nothing in the record or these results points to capacity. Not worth more card.

**U7.** CPU; nothing for the card. Its resolution limits every gain above: on tuning A2's P@2 interval is 0.446-0.552
(217 queries); held-out has 391 queries, so a difference under about 3-4 points of P@2 will not show [j].

jobs that leave the card idle gain less (section 3). Measured on his card: A4 (7B, nf4) at K30, window 10, 512 tokens a

**What every new form does to the choice of the best among many.** A1 and B1 stay the primary arms, named before any
held-out reading. Each form above is reported beside them and never chosen after the fact. D2 grows from 8 reported arms
to up to 12, so the chance that some arm beats A2 by luck grows; only a pre-named stack can be called a gain. If a new arm
is to enter a stack, name "A1+" (A1's features plus the new columns) before its held-out files exist, fit it the way U7
fits A1, and report it as a second stack. If U7 has already scored, the new arms get one scoring pass of their own,
named in advance, with no reselection of anything already frozen.

## 3. Lossless speed-ups of the arms as they are

Only changes that leave every number equal. Each needs one short check to count as shown; without it, it is not used.
1. **Several jobs on one card at once.** One trial, setting, fold or query shard per process, each with its own seed and
   batch shapes as now; a process computes the same kernels whatever runs beside it. This is the big one: U2's SweRank
   step (137M, batch 8, 256 tokens) uses about a quarter of even his card [c: about 6.6 TFLOP a step in 0.37 s], A4 decodes
   one window at a time, U6's small repositories run 0-19 s epochs. On a 96 GB card 4-8 such processes fit. Check: one
   short run alone and the same run beside others, loss trace and scores compared byte for byte. The move's U2 plan
   already does this one GPU a slot; several slots a GPU is the extension.
2. **U2's start-model baseline scored once, not in every stage job** (text S1: about 2 min a job). Same model, same data.
   Check: the cached and fresh baseline files are equal.
3. **Fewer checkpoint writes on the pod** (U2 every 480 s, `trainlib.py:71`; U6 at least every 10 min with bip-TGN states of
   1.5 GB). Saving reads the state and changes nothing; resume equality is already tested (U2 resume-test2, U6 tests).
4. **Gradient checkpointing off for Qwen3's LoRA training** (`trainlib.py:23`). It only trades memory for a recomputed
   forward pass (roughly a quarter to a third of a step) on the largest job of the move, Qwen3's sweep. Lossless only if
   50 steps with and without it give identical losses (dropout RNG is preserved by torch's checkpoint by default); if they
   differ at all, it stays on.

Not lossless, so not speed-ups of the existing arms: bf16 in place of nf4, a larger batch, TF32, flash attention in place
of sdpa, and A6's larger token budget per batch (U3 says it changes batching only; padding can move bf16 results, and no
equality check exists).

## 4. Ranking, what is not worth it, what stays untested

**Short list by expected gain per card-hour** (section 0): A4L 3.6 h, A4b 0.9 h, B2z 1.0 h, A6b 1.0 h, A5b 4.0 h; 10.5 h
in all. A4L and A4b share one card (about 88 GB) for about 3.6 h wall time and together use about the spare credit; the
rest need more credit. A4L ranks first because it is the only form with a recorded size effect on the weak spot (top
ranks); A4b is its control and is cheap; B2z is the only form aimed at zulip, which carries the D4 gate.

**Not worth it here:**
- A bigger graph network (more layers, wider): U5's 22 settings sit within 0.004; capacity adds nothing.
- A 7B or 8B embedder for D4's content feature or node features: text adds 0.002 in U5.
- Bigger or longer temporal models (DyGFormer, TGN): under the untrained baseline; counting wins in the record (C23, C71-C75).
- Fine-tuning an 8B embedder in place of A5: U2 at 0.6B already costs 7-16 PRO 6000 hours; about 13 times the step cost;
  the clinic has 189 pairs.
- More negatives or longer chunks for A4 and A6 beyond the authors' settings: no figure in the record.
- ULTRA now that it fits: its weak case is ours, and it needs a kernel build.
- HyDE query rewriting: no figure in the record; a reranker is the better use of a 7B.

**Stays untested** (priorities, not verdicts; one null closes no family):
- Fine-tuning the 7B reranker on our own history with LoRA: the record backs tuned rerankers (C15, C16, C18), but it is
  about 6 card-hours [c: about 10,000 windows at roughly 2 s] and invariant-heavy code; the next form after A4L.
- A listwise LLM reranker over B2's top 30 for D4: no record; the reranker was tuned for issue-to-function, not file-to-file.
- Several seeds for U5, U6, A4 and A5: they measure spread, not gain; cheap once item 1 of section 3 is in place.
- ULTRA, GraIL, HyDE, RotatE at its released settings.
- The 4-bit loss itself, unless A4b or A6b runs.
