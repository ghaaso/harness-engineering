## Summary
The three CI-failure systems (elastic-recheck, Jenkins Build Failure Analyzer, logreduce) confirm that a known-failure store is "one machine-matchable signature per entry, tied to a tracked defect, with a coverage metric". None documents a statistical false-positive control. Precision comes from authoring discipline. elastic-recheck has the best rule: a query that hits more than 10% of successful runs is too broad. It adds an optional second discriminator (test_ids) and retires entries on upstream fix status, with a dry-run cleanup. Jenkins BFA supplies a minimal schema (name, description, categories, regex indications, comment, modification history, lastOccurred) but no lifecycle or false-positive handling beyond a regex tester. logreduce is a novelty detector over baseline-good logs. It is not a known-failure matcher, but its canonicalisation recipe (strip hashes, UUIDs, dates, random prefixes) transfers. Develocity and Buildkite are hosted or commercial, so they are cited for design only. Develocity shows a retry-based discriminating oracle (FLAKY = fail then pass) and a prefix-override-the-model classification rule. KCS v6 supplies the lifecycle (WIP, Not Validated, Validated, Archived), template (Issue, Environment, Resolution, Cause, Metadata), many-to-many typed links, "reuse is review", flag-it-or-fix-it, and the metric hierarchy "reuse accuracy over reuse rate" (target above 90%). The staffed-org machinery (licensing, coaches, KDEs, audience promotion, self-service success) does not transfer. The volume-driven statistics also do not transfer, because ER's backend needed about 6 TB for 7 days of logs. KCS has no negative-results field, so the "failed approaches" field is our addition. The web search budget ran out after two searches, so Develocity's grouping mechanics and ER's published classification rate were not verified.

## Design implications
- (strong) Gate every write on a negative-corpus precision check. Run the entry's handles (canonicalised templates, regex or query) against known-good logs and unrelated historic failures, and reject or tighten an entry that fires on them. ER's own rule is a 10% ceiling on success hits, but with our small corpus use zero tolerance for the negatives we hold. This is the only documented write-time false-positive control in the prior art, and it costs the agent no extra tracking. [F02, F23]
- (strong) Every entry carries a required, discriminating verification check, not just a handle. ER's test_ids (log hit AND a specific test failed) and Develocity's fail-then-pass retry oracle are the two precedents. A symptom match is a candidate and only the check on current code is the verdict. [F03, F16]
- (strong) Invalidate on events tied to what the entry depends on (ER ties retirement to the upstream bug's fix status; we tie it to changed files, symbols, dependency versions and platform). Do not use ER's 'no hits in 10 days' rule. At tens to a few hundred entries, absence of hits is not evidence. When ER's Future Work still lists 'check whether resolved bugs return' as unbuilt, it shows even the precedent never closed the loop, so we need re-open and restore as a first-class transition. Archived (not deleted) with a restorable state follows KCS. [F05, F17]
- (strong) Keep the outcome history as a separate many-to-many link record between an entry (with its version) and the incident that used it, typed resolved/referenced/refuted, with a snapshot or version pointer of what was delivered. That table, and not a counter on the entry, makes reuse accuracy, hit/refute/miss rates and cost-saved analytics possible, and matches KCS's insistence that links be machine-usable. [F21, F20]
- (moderate) Use reuse accuracy (1 minus the refute rate) as the headline health metric and coverage miss rate (the ER 'unclassified' page, KCS known-vs-new) as the second. Report raw counts with denominators, because at a few writes per phase percentages and KCS's numeric targets (above 90%, above 60%, 85% new) are noise. [F04, F20, F21]
- (strong) Adopt 'reuse is review' and 'flag it or fix it' as the only maintenance process. Review happens at hit time through the verification gate, never as a scheduled sweep, which fits the ADHD constraint of less to track. An agent that verifies and finds a small error fixes the entry. An agent that cannot confirm flags it (state Not Validated or Refuted) and the flag escalates to a top-tier session or the owner. KCS requires that a flag triggers a workflow, not just a counter. [F19, F17]
- (moderate) Start from KCS's template and states and extend them. Keep Issue (verbatim error text plus the agent's paraphrase), Environment (machine-checkable: files, symbols, versions, platform), Resolution as numbered steps, one cause per entry (split otherwise), and metadata. Add what KCS lacks: a diagnosis path, a failed-approaches list with reasons, validity conditions, and the verification check. KCS has no negative-results field, so this is our addition, not prior art. [F18, F17]
- (moderate) Canonicalise machine text with the logreduce recipe before indexing or matching: strip long hex hashes, UUIDs, dates and random prefixes, and keep a stable token alphabet. Keep entries' raw verbatim symptom text alongside, as KCS says to quote error messages exactly. [F12, F18]
- (moderate) Do not adopt the infrastructure of these systems or their statistics. ER's backend needed about 6 TB per 7 days of logs and became a hosting burden. BFA needs Jenkins and Mongo for stats. Develocity and Buildkite are hosted. Our store should keep entries as git-tracked data with a locally rebuilt index and use a lightweight engine behind the abstraction layer, which the brief already requires. [F07, F10, F11, F15]
- (moderate) Novelty detection (logreduce) is a diagnosis aid at most. It answers 'what is unusual in this failed log', not 'have we seen this before', and its documented failure mode (extra debug content only in failed runs) means it floods with false positives. It should not sit in the retrieval path of a precision-first store. [F13, F14]
- (strong) KCS's staffed-organisation machinery does not transfer: licence levels, coaches, KDE, Process Adherence Review sampling, audience promotion, 90/0, and customer self-service success. Likewise skip BFA/ER-style per-job statistics dashboards. The agent-level analogue of licence is simple and already in the orchestration method: cheap agents may confirm or flag, only top-tier sessions validate a new entry or change its check. [F22, F20]
- (weak) The precedents document no refute-to-entry feedback loop and no confidence score on a hit. Our relevance-feedback and revise/retain loop therefore comes from case-based reasoning and KCS's reuse-is-review, not from CI classifier prior art, and should be justified from those sources. [F23, F19]

## Rejected paths
- Run elastic-recheck (logstash/OpenSearch) as the store or index — Designed for CI log volumes of terabytes. It is coupled to Gerrit, Launchpad and subunit2sql. OpenStack itself struggled to host and scale it. Far too heavy for one developer with a few hundred entries.
- Jenkins Build Failure Analyzer as the engine — Requires Jenkins (this project runs GitHub Actions). It has single-line regex first-match semantics, no documented lifecycle or refute loop, and statistics only via MongoDB.
- logreduce as the symptom matcher — It is a novelty detector, not a known-failure lookup. It needs a clean baseline of good runs, it floods with false positives when failed runs contain extra content, and the last release was January 2021.
- Hosted failure-analytics products (Develocity failure classification, Buildkite Test Engine) as the store — Hosted or commercial and data leaves owner-controlled machines, so disqualified for production use. Neither exposes a user-editable symptom-to-fix knowledge base in the pages read. Cited for design only.
- ER-style 'no hits in 10 days' time/traffic expiry — Needs thousands of events per day to be meaningful. With a few writes per phase, silence is expected for healthy entries. Invalidation must be driven by dependency change events.
- Adopt KCS wholesale (roles, licensing, audience promotion, PAR, AQI targets) — It is a process for a staffed support organisation. Only the lifecycle states, template, linking, reuse-is-review, flag-it-or-fix-it and reuse-accuracy emphasis transfer.
