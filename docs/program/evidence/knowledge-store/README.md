# Knowledge-store track — raw research outputs

Raw per-agent outputs of the knowledge-store research workflows (sweep, synthesis inputs, judge
panel, refuters, completeness critic), copied verbatim from the session scratchpad so the design
in `../../KNOWLEDGE-STORE-DESIGN-2026-11.md` rests on files in the repository. `*.json` is the
structured result, `*.md`/`*.txt` the rendered view. `manifest.sha256` lists every file's digest
at copy time (its own manifest, so it never conflicts with `../manifest.sha256`, which belongs to
the W1/W2 passes). These files are evidence, not state: nothing here is updated after the fact.

Subfolders: `sweep/` (the research lanes and re-checks), `panel/` (proposals and judgments), `refute/` (refuter verdicts and the completeness critic), `critique-2026-11-03/` (the owner's critique of v1.2 and its adjudication: verdicts, research, the check workflow's script, output and journal). `refute-v1.3/` (the refuter pass over the three mechanisms v1.3 introduced and the completeness critic over the v1.3 text: six verdicts, the critic, the workflow script, output and journal). Each subfolder's files are covered by the one `manifest.sha256` here.
