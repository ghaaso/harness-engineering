The ideas
Every harness idea we have had, whatever its results so far: 484 M ideas in 12 themes. Each one says the problem it is meant to solve, how it works, where it stands and what comes next. Ideas that were tried and taught us something, or that we parked, stay in the list with what we learned, so nobody has to rediscover them.
