Keep the machine up and the work safe
12 in use · 1 tested · 1 built · 14 designed · 4 ideas · 3 parked Work packages: WP1WP2a
Everything we build runs on one PC. Between 7 October and 10 November it powered off unexpectedly 24 times M. Of the 25 power-offs since 1 October, 15 M came with no Claude call in any project in the ten minutes before, so our own load alone does not explain them. The clinic testbed still lives only on this machine.
The ideas here keep work safe when the PC stops and bring it back without me: an off-machine copy, a resume list that brings sessions back two at a time, and an always-on sensor logger that will show why it powers off. Separate worktrees already pay: when the power went for 28 minutes on 13 November, all eight FixFlow lanes resumed from their own copies M. This theme is harness v2 phase 1, and I answered its first six decisions on 11 November; a full restart chain waits, because I chose to keep Docker’s start at sign-in off for now (D03).

CHK
Theme · 43 ideas
Fast, fair, trustworthy checks
21 in use · 4 tested · 2 built · 8 designed · 4 ideas · 4 parked Work packages: WP2aWP2bWP3
The check is where our biggest measured wins already are. Running tests in parallel took the clinic check from 855.2 s to 283.8 s M, tracing start-up took a 16-test run from 43 s to 11.5 s M, and the Linux container run takes 230.1 s against 367.2 s natively M.
What remains is making it the one gate everyone trusts: sized from measured memory, fair in its queue, visible while it runs, and never green when it tested nothing. FixFlow’s gate harness already carries several of these ideas into daily use; harness v2 phase 2 does the rest.

Review and fix rounds that converge
21 in use · 1 tested · 4 built · 17 designed · 12 ideas · 5 parked Work packages: WP3WP7WP9WP10a
In the clinic testbed a slice took 2.8 review rounds on average M, and 64 % of reviews sent the slice back M. Some fixes created new defects, some reports claimed more than was done, and in 5 of 6 high-risk merges nobody had read the final commit M.
The ideas here make review converge and make its verdicts checkable: a contract for each fix round, scripts that test claims, fresh reviewers that try to refute, and a merge queue that records who read what. Harness v2 phase 3 turns the strongest of them into six scripts that spend no tokens.
