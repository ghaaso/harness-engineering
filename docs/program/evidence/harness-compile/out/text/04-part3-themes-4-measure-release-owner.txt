Every number in this document carries a tag that says how sure we are of it. That habit, and most of the measurements in it, came from this theme.
The ideas here are how we measure and how we keep a test fair to the idea it tests: tallies from our own transcripts, replays on recorded work, held-out test sets frozen before the run, models of the whole system, and rules such as testing the strongest form of an idea against the strongest baseline.

For FixFlow the same theme means real Android builds and real devices. The lab emulator already boots on this PC with Android 14 and the lab app installed.
