## 1. How the sweep was done, and its stated limits

1. Purpose: it scored ideas against FixFlow's own weaknesses, not against "how to lay out instruction files". "The weaknesses and levers every idea was scored against" (MAIN:19); W1 is "`CLAUDE.md` is 390 lines, nearly twice the length at which adherence is known to fall; a trim is gated behind the knowledge-store pilot" (MAIN:23).
2. Size: "326 sources, 46 blind Sonnet readers, 1,987 idea cards deduplicated by code to 1,906, clustered into 838 claim families, each scored by two Opus reviewers with opposite lenses" (MAIN:36-38). Addendum of 2026-11-09 added 36 sources, 311 cards, "no disposition changed" (MAIN:49-50).
3. Evidence quality: "of 1,906 cards only 75 (4 %) carry a measured number" (MAIN:44-46). "Nothing survived whole. Every surviving adopt (24 families) and trial (53) is the refuter's narrowed version" (MAIN:41-43). Nothing was run: "Nothing here is installed, wired or adopted" (MAIN:5-6).
4. Gaps: "Slides and images were not read anywhere" (MAIN:525); the AGNTCon talk on verifiers "still has no recording or slides as of 2026-11-09; its promo was read, the talk was not" (MAIN:129-131); not read: "the primary studies behind W1's adherence figure and L2's heuristic" (MAIN:619-620); still unread: "Reply threads on X", LinkedIn, slides (MAIN:893-894).
5. Method limit: "The main session (Fable) ... read no source in full itself and scored no card" (MAIN:440-441); the inventory it started from is "a STARTING INDEX for the research session, not a finished sweep" (INV:3); some posts are "by Kyle Mistele (CTO), not Dex" (INV:9). "already-done" means only that "harness 1 contains the mechanism on paper. It says nothing about whether harness 2 practised it, or whether it worked there" (BRIEF:12).
