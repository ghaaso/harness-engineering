WHAT CHANGED, BY RULING AND REGISTER ID:
- C76 / O05-12 (reservation cap):
  - The cap ships OFF: media_max_live_reservations = 0, meaning no cap. With the cap at 0 the service takes no lock and runs no count (F5 too).
  - The lock, the count, the 429 + Retry-After and their tests stay, run with the cap set.
  - config.py and CLAUDE.md say when the cap may be switched on: after a phone release that treats our 429 as a free retry and waits for Retry-After, and that shows the pending-media list with Retry (O3-F5). The value must be far above what one phone's own failed attempts leave in 10 minutes.
  - media_require_declared_size stays OFF until a phone release declares the size; the comment says why (every upload would get a 422 and park).
  - /api/ops/health facts carry media_max_live_reservations and media_require_declared_size as they are in effect.
- C77 / T44-media-1 + O05-13 (finalize):
  - A finalize of a row already uploaded is an idempotent success. It returns the row unchanged, after the who-may-finalize check, and never reads, purges or deletes again. That holds on a closed job too.
  - Open (journaled): replacing the bytes inside the URL's 600 s until the ETag is recorded at finalize.
- C77 / O05-09, O6-F17, O08-F08 (ops lock):
  - One console.warn per engaged lock: ip=<JSON-quoted key> failures=<n> lock_seconds=<s>.
  - Never a password, token or body.
- C80 F1 / T44-media-1:
  - An oversized finalize on a CLOSED job purges the object and keeps the row pending (frozen evidence; the evidence-gaps report still shows the gap).
  - Chosen rule: "closed job", every phase, mirroring the freeze in delete. Why not "closing phase": the close gate counts closing rows only while the job is open, where deleting the oversized row is right; the gaps report reads closing rows of closed jobs only, and those are kept.
- C80 F4 / T44-media-1:
  - The service purges the object and raises MediaUploadOversizedError.
  - The router rolls back (the access_by_token claim and its claim event go), then deletes that one row by id while it is still pending, commits, and answers 413.
- C80 F2 / T44-media-2: an empty canary never passes. With candidates and no finalized key, the run aborts and logs why.
- C80 F3 / T44-media-2: every deletion, by the sweep or a finalize, logs one "media deletion:" line: source, outcome (row/object/both), media_id, job_id, phase and the server-built path. No free text.
- C80 per-page commit: sweep_stale_pending(on_page_done=...); the scheduled entry passes session.commit.
- C80 floor: SWEEP_MIN_AGE is 1 h. A lower setting is clamped with a warning; the boot does not fail.
