- O05-09 / O6-F17 / O08-F08 (ops-server exposure and login):
  - Per-IP backoff keyed on CF-Connecting-IP, falling back to the socket peer. Five free failures, then 30 s doubling to 15 min, with 429 + Retry-After.
  - A locked IP is refused before its body is read, and the lock is checked again after the body.
  - A success forgets the IP's failures. The map is bounded at 10k entries.
  - 16 KB body cap: a declared length over it is refused up front; a chunked body is refused as it crosses the cap (413).
  - requestTimeout 15 s, headersTimeout 10 s.
  - Boot refuses an OPS_SESSION_SECRET under 32 characters, and an OPS_PASSWORD that is unset or under 16 characters. The example placeholder is shorter than 16; the test reads it from the file. Neither value is ever printed.
  - ops sits only on opsnet (internal: true), shared with cloudflared, backend and web. Its health targets are internal URLs.
  - The tunnel template's commented ops. route carries a commented originRequest.access guard, marked VERIFY.
  - Tests: tests/ops-server.test.mjs (22 tests). compose-rules.mjs gains three rules.
- O08-F09: an unset Railway or Sentry row reports not_configured and does not degrade the rollup. Test: "is ok when every configured component is ok and Railway/Sentry are unset".
- O05-12 (media reservation):
  - Per-principal cap: media_max_live_reservations = 20 pending reservations within the 600 s URL lifetime. Past it: 429 + Retry-After.
  - The count is taken under pg_advisory_xact_lock(hashtext('media_reservation'), hashtext(created_by)).
  - A declared size_bytes over the ceiling gets 413 before any row exists. Otherwise it is signed into the PUT URL as its Content-Length (botocore puts it in X-Amz-SignedHeaders).
  - media_require_declared_size defaults OFF.
  - Tests: test_service.py, test_router.py, test_storage.py (real botocore), and test_media_hardening_integration.py (real Postgres, including the parallel overshoot).
- O05-13: finalize ownership was already on main (P7a, #191). This PR adds only the commit on the 413 path.
- T44-media-1: the finalize 413 commits its row delete before raising. Tests: test_complete_too_large_commits_the_row_delete, test_an_oversized_finalize_leaves_no_pending_row.
- T44-media-2: media-stale-pending-sweep, daily at 03:45 Asia/Karachi, threshold 48 h.
  - Keyset pages of 500, at most 20 per run; it reports truncated beyond that.
  - It decides each row by the R2 listing: bytes absent, the row goes; an oversized object, the object and the row go; bytes present within the ceiling, the row is kept.

  - It aborts on an unreadable listing, or when none of the 3 most recently finalized keys is listed (the wrong-bucket canary).
  - Storage calls run in asyncio.to_thread.
