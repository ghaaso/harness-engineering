Companion to `R:RULE-V2-DRAFT.md` (`R:` = `docs/program/evidence/round3-2026-11-18/`). One row per `V2-` number. Each
number stands in the draft beside the statement it numbers; where a statement recurs, the same number recurs, and the
section column lists every place.

- **Section**: 1 the answer; 2 row n, the table; 3 Step n, the rule; 3 Kn, the contradictions table; 4 sizes;
  5 Qnn, the measurement row; 6 build order; 7 not found.
- **Claim ids** carry their lane where two lanes share a letter: `c1:C07` is the check, `t1:C84` the tree.
- **Verdict, word for word.** Lanes c1, w1 and a1: the refuter's verdict in `R:c1-refute.md`, `R:w1-refute.md`,
  `R:a1-refute-1.md` (A01-A41) and `R:a1-refute-2.md` (A42-A82). Those claims files carry no verdicts; their reports
  fold CONFIRMED to confirmed, PARTIALLY to holds in part, UNVERIFIABLE to cannot be checked. Lane t1: the fold verdict
  in the table "Claim verdicts" of `R:t1-claims.md`. Where a statement rests on a report section or a refuter's
  "Missing" item and not on a claim, the column says so.
- **Use**: full, or in part (and which part). A refuted claim is used only to say what was refuted.
- **v2 verdict** (added in the fold, step 3): the fresh-context refuter's verdict on the statement as this file
  words it, from `R:v2-refute.md`, word for word: confirmed, holds in part, refuted, cannot be checked. **Fold**: what
  the fold did to the statement in the draft: kept, narrowed (to the part that held, or naming the part that failed),
  replaced, or left open with the measurement that would settle it.
