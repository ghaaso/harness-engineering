1. C80 F1: A CLOSED JOB KEEPS ITS ROW

Right for both: the gate reads closing rows only on open jobs and the gaps report only on closed ones. No path keeps the row on an open job; statuses never reopen.
- D3 MINOR: a closed job can still lose its row through a race. `job_closed` comes from the job read in `access_by_token`, before the rollback, and is never read again.
