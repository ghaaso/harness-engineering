B. THE CAP AGAINST A REAL DAY
- What the server counts: pending rows with `created_by` = the principal and `created_at >= now - 600 s`, refused when `live >= 20` (`features/media/service.py:156-163`; `core/config.py:60`; `core/storage.py:66`, `DEFAULT_UPLOAD_TTL = 600`). A row stops counting when it is finalized or when it turns 600 s old. VERIFIED-IN-CODE.
- How the phone leaves pending rows behind. Any attempt where the reservation lands but the PUT or the finalize does not leaves a row that counts for 600 s. Three ways: a failed or reset R2 PUT (`uploadMedia.ts:46-49`); a finalize that times out (`lib/api.ts:114`, `:134-136`); a reservation the server committed after the phone's 15 s timeout fired (`api.ts:114`). INFERRED from VERIFIED-IN-CODE branches.
- How many per drain pass: a PUT failure is a plain `Error`, so `connectivity`, so `free_retry`, and the drain stops (`lib/syncClassification.ts:195`; `pendingMedia.ts:235-236`): one leaked reservation per pass. An app 5xx on the finalize is `count_and_retry`; for media the drain then continues with the next entry (`BLOCKS_ON_RETRY.media = false`, `syncClassification.ts:187`; `pendingMedia.ts:232`): one leaked reservation per entry per pass. VERIFIED-IN-CODE.
- Live uploads in one arrival job: serial, error code, at least 2 condition photos, before video, voice and after video. That is at least 7 reservations, sent in parallel (`ArrivalCapturesScreen.tsx:78`, `:80-87`, `:89-96`, `:147-154`; `ArrivalAfterVideoScreen.tsx:78`; `ArrivalJobWizard.tsx:320`). VERIFIED-IN-CODE.
  - The server comment's premise is false against this code: "A phone reserves, PUTs and finalizes one file at a time and stops its drain on a failed PUT, so it holds one or two" (`core/config.py:58-59`). The wizard does not upload one file at a time, and leaked rows count whether the phone still "holds" them or not.
- What starts a drain pass: `drainAll` on mount, on every NetInfo event with `isConnected` true, and on app `active` (`lib/useDrainAll.ts:20`, `:28`, `:32`); every outbox save (`lib/outbox.ts:135-137`; `features/media/usePendingMediaDrain.ts:19-21`); a trigger that arrives mid-drain reruns the drain (`pendingMedia.ts:168-183`). VERIFIED-IN-CODE. INFERRED: coming back from `launchCameraAsync` fires `active`, so during the arrival wizard each capture is also one drain pass.
- What the phone does with our 429: it is not in `DEFINITIVE_4XX` and is not 401 or 413, so it is `server` (`syncClassification.ts:74`, `:196-199`), which is `count_and_retry` on the media row (`:164-170`). The drain adds 1 to `attempts` and moves on to the next entry (`pendingMedia.ts:228-233`). So while the cap is full, every eligible entry gets +1 on every pass. At `MAX_ATTEMPTS = 5` (`syncClassification.ts:80`, `:213-215`) the entry is parked with `failedReason` "server 429" (`pendingMedia.ts:124-136`, skipped at `:201`). Counts from earlier 5xx persist, so such an entry parks sooner. Retry-After is never read: `ApiError` carries `status` and `fromApp` only (`api.ts:83-99`), and a search of `technician-app/src` for `Retry-After` / `retry-after` / `retryAfter` finds nothing (VERIFIED-BY-COMMAND). The live wizard queues on a 429 rather than failing the slot (`features/jobs/arrival-job/evidenceUpload.test.ts:20`; not run).
- The numbers: the cap fills when the phone leaks at least 20 reservations within 600 s, about 2 per minute on average. Once full, 5 drain passes park every queued entry. A pass makes no new reservation while every request gets 429, so the cap frees about 600 s after the oldest leak. While PUTs keep failing, each freed slot is used and leaked again, so the cap stays full.
- When this is reachable (INFERRED, no device evidence): a weak-signal home visit (the small JSON reservation gets through and the multi-MB PUT does not; about 7 parallel live leaks plus about one drain pass per camera return, plus NetInfo connect events, can reach 20 within 10 minutes); any R2-side PUT failure, such as an R2 outage or wrong credentials after the host move (every phone leaks at its trigger rate).
- Before this PR, in the same conditions, the entries were kept and never counted. A failed PUT keeps the entry (`pendingMedia.test.ts:158-169`). So this is a new way to park honest evidence.
- On `main`, a parked entry is effectively lost to the technician. `listPendingMedia` and `pendingMediaCount` have no importer outside `pendingMedia.ts` (VERIFIED-BY-COMMAND; O3-F5 is still open). The wizard tile stays "queued" for ever, and there is no Retry or Discard.
- Answer: yes, 20 in 600 s can park honest evidence. How often in a real week is unknown.

- The declared-size 413 at reservation (`service.py:149`) is unreachable for the same reason. VERIFIED-IN-CODE.

F. FINDINGS
- S1, the cap parks honest evidence. Reachable with the next build from `main`: yes. How often in a real week: unknown, concentrated in weak-signal visits and in any R2 PUT failure. INFERRED. Smallest change, on the server in this PR: raise the default of `media_max_live_reservations` above what the phone's own leaks can reach (on the order of 100 or more), or make 0 mean "off" and ship it off until the phone treats our 429 as `free_retry` and honours Retry-After (the same seam-off pattern as `media_require_declared_size`).
