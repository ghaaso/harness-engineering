**Tried and could not break**
- **Read-only:** every statement is a SELECT and no ORM objects are used. The connection sets `default_transaction_read_only=on`, the transaction is `SET TRANSACTION READ ONLY` and checked with `SHOW` (`:403-411`), and `txid_current_if_assigned()` is checked before any file is written (`:1048`). `test_run_export_refuses_a_session_that_has_written` exercises that last check. `--seed-week` adds rows in memory only.
- **Host guard:** a remote URL was refused with exit 3, and the message does not echo the URL.
- **Money:** amounts stay `int` and are formatted with `divmod` (`:205`). No float or `Decimal` is used; negatives and `bool` are refused. The 2**53+1 round trip passed against Postgres.
- **Voids:** a void is its own `_x1` reversal with the lines swapped and the original named in its Reference. That matches the spec's "non-voided payments, voids"; `--voids pairs` gives the O-07 shape.
- **External IDs:** they come from the row's UUID only and are sorted by (date, ID). Two real exports against Postgres are byte-identical (`:212`). The form is Odoo-safe.
- **Pseudonymised tokens:** an HMAC under a secret key that is refused inside `--out`. The ledger `note`, the payment `void_reason` and customer fields are never selected. Payment rows carry job tokens only.
- **CSV:** formula leads are prefixed with `'` (`:806-808`). Amounts are unsigned, so a negative number is never touched. The `csv` module handles quoting, and output is UTF-8 with `\r\n`.
- **Empty-week refusal:** at least one live payment, one void and one live ledger row are required (`:761-769`), with exit 4 and nothing written.
- **Layer rules:** the script imports only `app.shared.tenancy`.
