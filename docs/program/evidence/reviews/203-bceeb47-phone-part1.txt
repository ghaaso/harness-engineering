C. M4: DOES "TECHNICIAN OF THAT INSTALL" ANSWER IT?

Mostly yes. Clips upload under whoever is signed in, after the outbox in each drain pass (`drainAll.ts:11-13`; no owner check in `pendingMedia.ts` `drainOnce`). Widening `phone_techs` to every owner and uploader in all of the install's manifests and after-reports covers any technician who later sends a report or owns a row.

The sequence that still ends `missing`:
1. A third technician, t3, signs in on the drain phone after the last report and owns no rows.
2. That drain pass uploads the clip as t3.
3. t3 signs out before an automatic after-report goes. The automatic report is limited to one per 5 minutes (`afterSend.ts` `AFTER_AUTO_MIN_INTERVAL_MS`).
4. The next report is sent by t1.
The upload's `created_by` is t3, who is in no document, so the clip reads `missing`, exit 3.
- Likelihood: very low in a supervised drain with one phone.
- Is the lane's proposed rule safe? It does not add a new kind of false accounting. The existing risk grows slightly: a co-technician's own-phone upload of the same job/phase/type after the receipt could account for this phone's lost clip. That risk already exists with `phone_techs`.
- A cheap extra: the actors of `queue_discard` traces carrying that install id.
