- M1. The tunnel's answer during a boot loop. Runbook :783 (3.6a) and :3000 (12.4) say "the tunnel answers 503". The runbook's own 5.9 (:1217) says a stopped backend in normal routing answers a 5xx, "expected `502`". The 503 is the tunnel's catch-all for hostnames it has no route for; a route whose backend is down gets a gateway error from cloudflared. The 502 itself is INFERRED; the contradiction is in the text. Fix: "a 5xx (502 expected, 5.9)" at both places.
- M2. R10.4's "down past 05:00 (the job's grace is an hour)" is false. VERIFIED-BY-COMMAND with APScheduler 3.11.3: `CronTrigger(hour=4).get_next_fire_time(None, 04:30)` returns the next day's 04:00, and a job added after its time has passed does not run. A backend not running at 04:00 skips that day, even if it is back by 04:10. The one-hour grace only covers a late run inside a scheduler that is already running. The sentence comes from the code comment at `backend/app/main.py:214-218`, which is wrong too (backlog). Fix: "the backend was not running at 04:00 shop time".
- M3. R10.2's Check misses the usual rewrite. Runbook :3825-3826 counts "nothing while today's head seq is at or above <n>" as a rewrite. The chain has no gaps, so a missing row at <n> with today's head at or above <n> is already a `seq_gap` (`chain.py:470-480`). The realistic rewrite (cut the tail, recompute) gives a lower head and no row, and the Check does not flag it. Fix: "a different hash, or no row at <n>, means rewritten (outside the restore case below)".
- M4. No routine step performs the anchor check. R10 says "the kept mails are the check against that". But R0.7 only compares the seq with yesterday's, and R10.2 (comparing a kept mail's hash) is reached only from BROKEN, a lower seq or no mail. A rewrite that recomputes the chain with the same or a higher seq passes the verify and R0.7 every day. Fix: R0.7 adds a periodic R10.2 against an older kept mail.
- M5. 3.6a's Expected says "the five anchor names". The Do says to uncomment the template's anchor block, which includes `FIXFLOW_SMTP_PORT=587` and `FIXFLOW_SMTP_STARTTLS=true` (`backend.env.example:40-46`). Done that way, line 1 of the Check prints seven names (VERIFIED-BY-COMMAND). Fix: say seven, or say to delete those two lines.
- M6. 6.9's "Its ids and times are minted at this boot" is half wrong. The backfill's revision ids are uuid5 of the job id; only the revisions' `created_at` is the boot's `now()` (`0048...py:209`, `:240`, `:256`). The payment entries come first in the chain and hash the same as production's; the head differs from the first revision entry on.
