# A10 option 2: Two ladders, one rung per stage
Lens: predictions first. This option departs from it: arms follow the designs as built, not the refutations.

**Mechanism.** Each design is trained as argued, stage by stage. Each rung adds one stage on the frozen rungs below, so it differs from the rung beneath in one decision and the top rung is the whole design. The cascade makes the ladder the training itself.
*C1 ladder:* b_k; L1 = linear stage 1 over x̃; L2 = L1 plus typed, gated propagation with φ, J absent; L3 = L2 with J (C1.r1 whole).
*C3 ladder:* b_k; T = trees over the record with per-kind residuals; T2 = T plus the tuned reader with R_ex in its head (C3.r1 whole).
*Baselines:* D12's list, same questions.
*Cards:* A trains L3; B trains L2, then scores; C trains T2's reader, sized by C3's N = C/(6LP) at one card (about 1.3×10^8 at 7.5×10^6 pairs). L1 and T first, on one shared subsample.
*Reading:* per repository, weighted by 1/π of inclusion, paired differences, intervals by resampling clusters in strata (D12). Choices on validation; test scored once.
*Predictions.* Inside a ladder each rung beats the one below on its design's segment: L2 over L1 among right files within two typed hops; L3 over L2 and T2 over T among right files with no joint change and no two-hop path. Across ladders, only where the Cannot lists differ: L3 beats T2 among files reached only by a typed three-hop route; T2 beats L3 among files below K for L1 but inside T's shortlist. Each "Refuted if" item is read at its rung.

**Needs from other parts.** C1 and C3 whole. A9: one card for L3 at about one epoch (C1: about two hours per epoch on three cards); both stage 1s before or in hour one; vectors shared.

**Can.** Test each design in its strongest form, with its interactions: C1's linear stage 1 with the U x̃ it leans on, C3's reader with the record in its head. M8 in part: one decision per rung inside a ladder.

**Cannot.** Attribute a difference between top rungs to one decision: L3 and T2 differ in stage-1 form, relation form and what the reader trains. Remove order effects: a rung's gain is measured given those below. Run the single-column swaps (H, event kinds, merged kinds); processors could add them. Nearest veto W12: two argued designs are not a sweep; M8's one decision per arm holds only inside a ladder, so it ranks lower there.

**Rests on.** D 7.2, D 7.3, D 3.2, N 1.1, N 9.4 (a conjunction needs joint inputs, so a stage judged without its partner is not the design); assumption: the top rungs' learning curves do not cross after one epoch; count first: validation questions per segment.

**Refuted if.** A rung's gain changes sign when its stage is trained directly on b_k, without the rungs below: the order, not the decision, carried it.
