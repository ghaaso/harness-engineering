# A9 option 2: materialised rows, stages trained apart
Lens: one model trained end to end on all the cards. Departs: most of both designs is exact and computed once (N 14.4); only readers need a card.

**Mechanism.** *Once, before rental, on processors:* one time-ordered pass per training repository, the store cut at each t_q (D 9.4), writes for each question the rows R(q) whole plus A7's Poisson sample of negatives with π_c stored, carrying the union of both designs' exact columns in 8-bit bins. The shared frozen encoder embeds chunks and event messages once. Stage 1 of each design is fitted there out of fold on two halves of the training clusters (C1's linear s_1, C3's trees and per-kind residuals). Shortlists (C1's top K and K_J, C3's M ∪ K) are then frozen, and each pair's token sequence is written. Baselines scored on the same questions.
*On the cards:* three jobs that never synchronise. Card 1: C1's stage 2 (propagation, φ, adapters) on frozen out-of-fold s_1, loss L2 over K plus L1 on s_1 + [c ∈ K] s_2 with s_1 fixed. Cards 2 and 3: C3's reader, data-parallel. Fixed shapes: an epoch costs pairs × 6NL / rate, known before rental.
*Sampling:* repositories and questions drawn as in D11 with probabilities kept; negatives fixed in the rows; C1's neighbours Poisson with π_u on the exact edge weight, weighted 1/π_u.
*Hours:* 0–0.5 measure step time and memory per job; 0.5–7 the jobs, reader-changing A10 arms cut from their own job; 7–8 validation, test once, teacher scores saved. Column or stage-1 arms run on processors. *12 GB form:* as option 1, a student distilled on the PC from saved scores; C3's s_0+s_1 need no card.

**Needs from other parts.** C1: accepts a frozen stage 1 (its joint L1 is lost); stage 2 on one card, 6.5 card-hours, about one epoch by its figures. C3: as designed; its reader gets 13 of its 15 assumed card-hours. A7: π_c and the negative sample. A10: which arms touch a reader.

**Can.** M2 by construction (exact columns never sampled). M4 by construction: D13's cut-against-whole test runs on stored rows, before any card time. M7 open on counts. M8 in part: one row set for arms and baselines; most arms cost no card. A failed job loses one card.

**Cannot.** Let stage 1 learn from stage 2: a right file s_1 ranks below K is never corrected, in either design. Refresh negatives each epoch: every pass sees one sample, so the variance of Z is not averaged. A change to K after validation means rebuilding shortlists and token files on processors. Rows must be moved to the rented machine: ~3×10^5 questions × |R(q)| ~300 × 1.1 KB ≈ 100 GB (count first). Nearest veto W9: shortlists use out-of-fold s_1, so no stage reads its own training fit.

**Rests on.** N 14.4, N 14.2, N 14.3; D 3.2, D 2.9, D 9.4, D 6.4; record: FACTS-MODELS-SO-FAR.md (the tree ranker over exact pair features leads). Assumption: 1.5×10^14 effective operations per card. Count first: |R(q)| per repository, processor hours for the pass, recall at K and M.

**Refuted if.** C1 trained jointly (option 1) beats C1 staged beyond the intervals per unseen repository; or re-drawing negatives per epoch moves per-repository ranks beyond the intervals.
