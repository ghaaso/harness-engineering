# A10 option 1: Shared trunk, one swap per arm
Lens: predictions first.

**Mechanism.** Arms only where C1.r1 and C3.r1 disagree or name their own refutation: a shared trunk plus one swap, same examples, read on the segment its prediction names.
*Trunk:* the exact record x̃ (A3 to A6 columns, typed path counts), offset b_k, A7's loss, π and weights, one training subsample with known probabilities (D 3.2), out-of-fold stage-1 offsets.
*Baselines:* D12's list and b_k, same questions.
*Stage-1 arms* (quarter subsample, about one card-hour): T = trees over x̃ with per-kind residuals (C3). One swap each: linear map (C1); kinds merged; no H; no event-kind columns; no walks or echo; raw logs; uniform uncorrected negatives; change questions only.
*Stage-2 arms* on T's frozen offset, one card each, equal card-hours (about 5.5):
- A0: C1's head at L = 0 (marks, φ, U x̃, gate); then A0 without gate.
- A: A0 plus C1's three typed, gated layers.
- B: joint reader on the shortlist, encoder frozen, adapters trained (C1's J); head reads [J; R_ex].
- C: B with all encoder weights trained (C3's s_2); same encoder, pairs, head.
*Reading:* per repository, weighted by 1/π of inclusion; paired differences; intervals by resampling clusters in strata (D12). Refuted when the 90 % interval on its segment lies on the wrong side of zero. Choices on validation; test scored once, never to choose.
*Predictions:* P1 T beats b_k per question kind. P2 linear within T's interval on recall at K, else C1's stage 1 falls. P3 A beats A0 among right files within two typed hops (C1 yes, C3 no; CRITERIA section 4) and those reached only by a typed three-hop route. P4 B beats T among right files with no joint change and no two-hop path. P5 C beats B there (C3) or not (C1). P6 each T swap loses on its named segment. P7 no larger lead for B or C on repositories created before the encoder's cut-off.

**Needs from other parts.** C1: head, layers, J. C3: record, trees, out-of-fold offsets, reader head. A9: vectors before or in hour one; the subsample; one card per stage-2 arm; the largest graph in 96 GB; half an hour to score.

**Can.** M8 by construction: one swap per arm, predictions that can fail. M7 open on counts.

**Cannot.** Train either design whole, or propagation with reader: an interaction stays unseen; C1's linear stage 1, which leans on U x̃, is judged without it. At one card C3's N = C/(6LP) gives about 1.3×10^8 parameters at 7.5×10^6 pairs, so a loss of C does not refute tuning at 0.35 B. Few error questions may leave P4 to P6 unreadable. Nearest veto W12: arms come from named disagreements, not a sweep.

**Rests on.** D 7.2, D 7.3, D 3.2, D 6.1, N 1.1; record: FACTS-MODELS-SO-FAR.md; count first: pilot spread per repository (D2), validation questions per kind and segment.

**Refuted if.** Arms differing in one swap disagree in sign between validation and test on a named segment.
