# A10 option 2: fractional factorial over the decisions
Lens: departs from the ladder: arms differ in several decisions; each effect is a contrast across half the arms.

**Mechanism.** The floor and the full-scale tops are the same as in option 1. Below the tops, every decision is a factor with two levels on one chassis per stage.
- C1 chassis: an eighth of the training clusters (known π, D 3.2), J frozen. Six factors: A a_k, B U x̃, C gate, D typed or merged, E J, F offset (C1's linear stage, or C3's out-of-fold trees with C3's shortlist). 16 runs, 2^(6-2), E = ABC, F = BCD. This is resolution IV: main effects are clear of two-factor interactions.
- C3 reader at an eighth: R_ex in the head × gate, a full 2^2.
- C3 trees: five column blocks, a full 2^5, 32 fits on processors.
- Effect: Δ_j = mean over + runs − mean over − runs, taken per repository, then averaged with 1/π weights and cluster-resampled intervals (D12). Interaction: AB = ½(Δ_A|B+ − Δ_A|B−). Segments are named before the run, as in option 1.
- Card-hours, of 24: the tops 4.5 each; C1 16 × 0.375; C3 4 × 0.6; scoring 3; 3.6 spare.

**Needs from other parts.** From C1: each decision can be switched off alone and training still works; stage 2 takes an outside offset. From C3: the out-of-fold offsets; a switchable head and gate. From A9: the eighth sample; the epoch time at an eighth.

**Can.** Measure redundancy. U x̃ and the gate may each cost nothing alone and something together: the ladder calls both useless, here BC is measured. F asks whether the cross-design question depends on the propagation decisions. Every run feeds every effect, eight runs per side. M8 in part: baselines, predictions and segments by construction, and each effect is one decision, but an arm is not one decision away from another.

**Cannot.** Three-factor interactions are aliased with main effects. If a_k helps only when U x̃ and the gate are both present, E absorbs it. Effects are assumed additive, which a top-ten measure may break. A degenerate run (no marks, no types and no J) contaminates every effect it enters. No single run is a readable design. A gain that shows only at full scale is hidden. Nearest veto: W12; the factors are the designs' named decisions, not a sweep.

**Rests on.** assumption: the standard two-level fractional factorial (aliases fixed by the generators; resolution IV keeps main effects clear of two-factor interactions). It is borrowed whole from outside the learning files. Also N 1.1; D 3.2, D 7.3; DATA-CHOICES D12; record: FACTS-MODELS-SO-FAR.md (removing the graph cost nothing, which a hidden interaction can produce). count first: card-hours of an eighth epoch; validation questions per segment.

**Refuted if.** Every interaction lies inside its interval, so the ladder reads the same more simply; or a degenerate run moves a main effect beyond its interval.
