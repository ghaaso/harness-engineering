# A9 option 3: one frozen reader shared by both
Lens: staged: compute and store first what is exact or frozen, then train the learned part.

**Mechanism.** S0 to S2 as in option 1 (exact record, frozen vectors, out-of-fold s_1 for each design). Then one cross-reader is trained once, on the union of both designs' shortlists, against the fixed offset b_k, which has no fitted part and so needs no folds. Its last vector r(q,c) and score are cached for every union pair. Each design then trains only what sits above it: C3's s_2 head reads [r; R_ex] with per-kind residuals d_j, D_j; C1's φ reads r in place of J, and its propagation trains on it.

Cards A, B, C; rate assumed 1.5×10^14 until measured:
- h0–0.5: confirm 96 GB; measure the rate and C1's step.
- h0.5–1.5: C3 trees, data-parallel; shortlists written.
- h1.5–4.5, all three: the reader (9 card-hours, 4.9×10^18; at C3's 7.5×10^6 pairs plus C1's extra, N ≈ 2×10^8 by N = C/(6LP), against about 1.2×10^8 per design in option 1).
- h4.5–5, all: r cached; teacher scores kept.
- h5–7.5, B and C: C1 propagation on cached r. A: C3 head and residuals (h5–6), then baselines and validation scoring.
- h7.5–8: settings on validation, test once.
Card-hours: reader 10.5 shared, C1 5, C3 4, setup and scoring 4.5. Memory: the reader with optimiser a few GB plus activations; r at 2×10^7 pairs × 512 half-precision numbers, about 20 GB.
12 GB form: exact stages on processors; the reader itself fits at half precision (0.4 GB); a smaller one is distilled on the PC from the teacher scores.

**Needs from other parts.** C3: its s_2 becomes a head over a frozen reader; its per-kind adapters inside the encoder become head residuals. C1: J replaced by r; its adapters do not train. A10: an arm that reads the comparison as "above the reader" only. A7: b_k exact.

**Can.** Give each design a reader about 1.7 times larger at the same hours. Make the comparison isolate the decision that separates C1 from C3: an exact record read by trees against learned propagation. M2 and M4 by construction, as option 1. M7 open on counts.

**Cannot.** Aim the reader at what s_1 misses: trained against b_k, it spends capacity on files the trees already rank. Counterexample: a change question whose right partner the bank puts second; the reader learns it again. Form the conjunction of text and record inside the encoder (C3's change 2 of A2): only in the head, over a fixed r. Tell the readers of C1 and C3 apart. Nearest veto W9: b_k fixed, settings on validation.

**Rests on.** N 14.4, N 1.1; D 6.4, D 9.4, D 13; assumption: a vector trained against b_k carries what a head needs to add the record's conjunctions; count first: union shortlist pairs, tokens per pair.

**Refuted if.** A design trained with option 1's own reader, at fewer hours, beats its shared-reader form beyond the intervals on right files below s_1's top ten.
