# A5 option 3: per-repository lag hazard, counted at asking
Lens: exact relational computation first; what can be computed exactly from the store relative to the question is computed, and a learned ranker reads it.

**Mechanism.** The decay curve is neither fixed nor trained: it is counted from the repository's own past before t_q. For every main-line unit u visible before t_q, every file s in u, and every partner c that changed with s before u, let b be the lag bucket (in units) since their last joint change. Count

N_R(b) = such (u, s, c) with c in u;  D_R(b) = all such (u, s, c);  h_R(b) = (N_R(b) + alpha h_0(b)) / (D_R(b) + alpha).

h_R(b) is the chance, in this repository, that a partner last seen together b units ago changes when s changes. Every observation lies at a time already passed, so no outcome is censored and nothing at or after t_q is read. h_0 and alpha are set on training repositories and times only (D 6.4); they carry a short history. Counting by the size of the earlier joint unit gives h_R(b, n). Columns for the ranker: H(s,c), the sum over joint units e of h_R(lag(e), n_e), and h_R at the lag of the last joint change. Old observations can be down-weighted inside the counts so the curve follows drift (D 7.4). Counts update in one pass in time.

**Needs from other parts.** A3: combining over starting files. A1: relative scaling. A7: a ranker reading these with option 1's columns if both are kept. A9: the cost of partner lists per unit (hub files).

**Can.** Give each unseen repository its own decay, non-monotone shapes included (the release-cycle pair: h_R is high at the cycle's lag), with nothing per repository learned. M3 by construction: a statistic of the repository computed at asking, not an identity or a fitted weight. M4 by construction, as option 1. M2: H is a count weighted by a curve; the exact decayed count is option 1's column.

**Cannot.** Separate coupling from churn: a file changed in most units gets a high h_R at every lag, so the candidate's own rate must be given beside it. Estimate the curve on a short or quiet history: at 10 units D_R is tiny and h_R is h_0. Say anything about a pair's first joint change: it describes repetition, not first contact (30, 66 and 21 % of right files had none; that is A6's). Nearest veto W9: h_0 and alpha come from training repositories and times only. W3: no vector per repository; the curve is recomputed from data at every t_q.

**Rests on.** D 9.1, D 6.4, D 7.4, D 3.2; N 9.5; FACTS: the share of right files with no earlier joint change. count first: D_R(b) per lag bucket at 10, 100 and 1,000 units of history, and how far h_R differs between repositories against within one over time.

**Refuted if.** Across validation repositories the h_R curves differ from h_0 no more than their own resampling spread; or adding H to option 1's columns moves no per-repository rank beyond the intervals.
