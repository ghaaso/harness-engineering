# F2: One shared reader, a two-by-two above it
Parents: C1.r1, C3.r1, A9.b.3, A10.b.1

## What is built
**C1.r1 (L+P).** Exact: the time-cut view, every reach, count, hazard and text column x̃ for every file alive, the offset b_k. Learned: a linear stage 1 over x̃ that ranks every file, then, for its top K, typed directed propagation (three layers, sums with no 1/|N_r|, shared bases, marked roots a_k, a per-channel gate that is the exact count at zero, U x̃ in the start state). For the top K_J a joint reading of question and file enters through φ. One score ranks every file; outside K the second term is zero.
**C3.r1 (T+R).** Exact: the same view and a pair record R_ex (typed path counts up to two links, the decay bank split by event kind, the counted lag curve, reach and echo). Learned: gradient-boosted trees over R_ex as stage 1 with per-kind shallow residuals, then on a shortlist a small head over [joint reading; R_ex] with per-kind residuals and the gated event sum.
**What differs.** Two decisions (A10.b.1): stage 1 linear (L) or trees (T); stage 2 a head over [r; x] (R) or propagation with marks (P).
**Built once and shared.** The store cut at t, the candidates with folder slots, b_k, the union x of both column sets, A7.c.1's loss and negatives, the frozen chunk and event vectors, and one cross-reader trained once against b_k whose last vector r(q,c) is cached for every pair in the union of all shortlists (A9.b.3).

## What is computed once and stored
1. **Store to view (processors).** The loader cuts the store at each asking time and asserts nothing served is dated at or after it (D6, D13; D 9.4).
2. **Frozen vectors.** One code-and-text encoder (0.1 to 0.3 B class) embeds each chunk once per content hash and each event's message plus first diff chunk once per commit (C1.r1 A1). Added, placement: computed on the PC's card before the rented window, because the content is public and C1.r1's own count (about 2 h on three large cards at 2×10^10 tokens) makes it a multi-day job on the PC, not a card-hour cost (assumption: the PC card's rate is about a sixth of one large card's). count first: distinct content-hash tokens the examples read, and event tokens. A leftover goes to h0 to 0.5.
3. **The union record x (processors, rented machine).** One time-ordered pass per repository: C1.r1's columns and C3.r1's R_ex, reach by sparse products (O(E) each), echo, BM25 with idf over files alive at t, hazard curves. Stored per row for R(q) whole plus the Poisson sample with known π_c (A7.c.1). Saved before any card hour: rows, π_c, weights w_q, flags.
4. **Repository graphs (processors).** Per repository, the typed graph with event nodes and timestamps, for P to mask by t on the card.
5. **On the cards and saved:** out-of-fold s_1 for L and T (h1.5), the union shortlist, r and the reader's teacher scores (h5), each arm's checkpoints and scores.

## The plan for the three cards, hour by hour
Cards A, B, C, taken as 96 GB each; rate assumed 1.5×10^14 effective until measured (A9.b.3). 24 card-hours in all.
- **h0 to 0.5, all.** Confirm 96 GB; measure the rate, one reader step and one P step on the largest repository graph. These fix N for the reader and the share rule below.
- **h0.5 to 1.5, all.** T: three fits data-parallel (two halves of the training clusters for out-of-fold offsets, one on all), about 0.85×10^8 rows of about 1,100 eight-bit columns per card (C3.r1). L: the same three fits, linear, minutes, on card A. Saved: out-of-fold s_1 per row, both shortlists (L's top K and top K_J; T's top M united with the text channels' top K; and T's own top K and K_J for T+P).
- **h1.5 to 4.5, all.** The reader, one sequence of 512 tokens per pair (question 256; path and best chunk 192; latest messages, sizes and ages before t 64, C1.r1 A2), trained with A7.c.1's loss on logits b_k + reader score over the union shortlist. Size N = C/(6LP), C = 9 card-hours: at about 7.9×10^6 pairs, N ≈ 2×10^8 (A9.b.3). Memory: weights with optimiser about 3.2 GB at 16 bytes per parameter, plus activations; far under 96 GB.
- **h4.5 to 5, all.** r cached for every union pair: about 2×10^7 pairs × 512 half-precision numbers, about 20 GB, on disk and in host memory; teacher scores kept.
- **h5 to 7.25, card B: T+P. Card C: L+P.** Each loads one repository graph at a time, batches its questions, 1 to 4 GB per question (C1.r1). Stage 1 is frozen out-of-fold; P's losses L1 over every file (s_2 zero outside K) and L2 exact over K. **h7.25 to 7.5:** each scores validation.
- **h5 to 7.5, card A.** h5 to 6: the R heads for L+R and T+R, the per-kind residuals of both stages (tree rounds per kind on validation). h6 to 7.5: baselines B and b_k (mostly processors), validation scores of B, L, T, L+R, T+R.
- **h7.5 to 8, all.** Settings on validation repositories; the test scored once for every arm.

**When a step runs long.** Every card job checkpoints (reader every 30 min, P every 20 min) and has a hang watchdog (added, from the owner's standing rule on card jobs). The reader stops at h4.5 at its last checkpoint. P stops at h7.25. If the rate measured at h0 is lower than assumed, N is recomputed by the formula before h1.5 and the next smaller qualifying reader is taken. If trees are not done at h1.5, the per-kind residuals move to card A at h5 (they are already there). If one P epoch on one card exceeds two hours (A10.b.1's Cannot; C1.r1 estimates under three hours at 3×10^5 questions), both P arms train on the same share of training repositories, drawn with known probability (D 3.2).

**Changed to fit.**
1. C1.r1's joint pass with adapters trained through φ becomes the cached r (A9.b.3). Reason: the reader is trained once, at about 1.7 times the size each design would get alone. What is cut: end-to-end training of the reader through C1's propagation.
2. C3.r1's per-kind adapters inside the encoder become residuals in the head (A9.b.3). Reason as 1.
3. C1.r1 trains stage 1 jointly with stage 2. Here L is fitted first, out of fold, and frozen. Reason: the union shortlist must be fixed before the reader starts at h1.5; the same rule then applies to L and T alike, so the L/T contrast is one decision.
4. Both stage 1 forms read the union x (A10.b.1). C1 therefore gets C3's path types and event-kind bank, C3 gets C1's columns. Reason: otherwise L against T mixes the model form with the column set.
5. Added: frozen vectors computed on the PC before the window (above).
6. Added: P runs 2.25 h per arm, not 2.5 h for one design on two cards (A9.b.3), because the two-by-two needs both P arms.

## The comparison
**Arms, same questions and same candidates, all above one reader:**

| Arm | What it is | Differs from its neighbour in |
|---|---|---|
| B | D12's baselines per kind, and b_k alone | - |
| L | linear stage 1 | B: a learned stage 1 |
| T | trees stage 1 | L: the form of stage 1 |
| L+R | L, head over [r; x] | L: stage 2 |
| T+R | = C3.r1 | T+R vs L+R: stage 1 |
| L+P | = C1.r1 | L+R: stage 2 form |
| T+P | trees, propagation | T+R: stage 2 form; L+P: stage 1 |

**Predictions, each on a segment named now (each can fail):**
- L: ahead of B in every tier and kind, on unseen repositories after the cut-off.
- T: ahead of L where two columns must combine: bulk-flagged repositories; trace depth with counts for errors.
- L+R and T+R: ahead of their stage 1 on right files with no joint change and no two-hop path, most for task words; within the interval where joint history exists.
- L+P and T+P: equal to their R twin overall; ahead only where a two-hop path runs through a middle file or event whose text matches the question.
- Interaction: T's lead over L is smaller under P than under R, since U x̃ lets P form conjunctions.

**Reading.** Per repository first, then the mean weighted by 1/π, intervals by resampling clusters inside strata (D12). A contrast counts if its interval on unseen repositories after the cut-off excludes zero. Segments: question kind and tier; history length; joint history or a code link to the right file, or neither; bulk-flagged; repositories created after the reader's cut-off; cold start at 10, 100 and 1,000 commits (D 7.3). The smallest arm whose interval overlaps the best arm's goes to the 12 GB form.

**Changed to fit.**
1. The reader trains against b_k (A9.b.3), not on T's residual. Reason: it removes A10.b.1's Cannot "L arms read vectors aimed at T's misses"; the price is A9.b.3's Cannot (capacity spent on files trees already rank).
2. Hours: P arms h5 to 7.25 on one card each, not h4 to 7, since h4.5 to 5 caches r.
3. Added: if the share rule fires, the R heads are trained a second time on the same share (minutes on card A), so that P against R stays like with like; the full-data R arms are reported beside them.
4. Added: every head's early stop and λ_j are set on validation repositories, whose r is out of sample. The reader saw the training pairs' labels, so r on training rows is in sample; an over-trusted r then shows as an early stop, not as a hidden gain. The test reading is unaffected: it is on unseen repositories.
5. A9.b.3's own test (a design with its own reader at fewer hours) is not an arm: a second reader does not fit in 24 card-hours.

## The smaller form for a 12 GB card
The arm picked by the rule above. Exact columns, b_k, trees and the linear stage run on processors. The reader at half precision is about 0.4 GB, so it runs on the shortlist on the card. A P arm runs one question at a time, the repository graph in processor memory, only the top-K neighbourhood on the card (C1.r1). If the reader is too slow on the PC, a smaller one is distilled there from the stored teacher scores (A9.b.3); that is training and needs his word.

## What must be counted before any run
1. Distinct content-hash tokens and event tokens the examples read: decides the frozen encoder's size class and whether vectors fit on the PC before the window.
2. |R(q)| and rows per question by repository, and the union column count after dropping unsupported path types: decides whether 2.5×10^8 rows fit the three cards and the Poisson rate κ.
3. Processor hours for the union record: decides whether it is built on the rented machine beforehand or on the PC.
4. Recall of right files at K, K_J, M and the text channels' top K, per repository on validation: decides the shortlist sizes.
5. Union shortlist pairs and tokens per pair: with the measured rate, decides N.
6. Edges per repository, the largest against 96 GB: decides whether P can read every repository or a drawn share.
7. Questions per kind, training and validation: decides per-kind residuals or the fallback to zero.
8. At h0: the rate and one P step: decides N and the share rule.
9. Validation questions after the reader's cut-off: decides whether M4's claim is open or by construction.

## Against the sheet
- **M1, by construction for change and error; for task words inside the shortlist, in part outside.** Marks a_k, U x̃, r and every column are functions of (Q, c) (N 9.4, N 9.8, N 9.10). Outside the shortlist the cosine column is two vectors computed apart, never the only column.
- **M2, by construction.** The decayed one-over-size count is exact in x and is b_k for a change; trees and sums keep it; P has no 1/|N_r|; the gate is neutral at zero; sampling is of candidates and of neighbours with known π, never of the exact counts (N 9.5; D 2.10).
- **M3, by construction for identities and scale; open on a test.** No identity per file or repository; amounts are quantiles and repository clocks; L = 3 and d ≤ 256 (N 8.4, N 13.7; D 8.1). Open: the reader recalling public repositories, tested on those created after its cut-off.
- **M4, by construction for data and settings; open on count 9.** One cut view; every learned step on training repositories and times; settings on validation (D 6.2, D 6.4, D 9.4).
- **M5, by construction; in part for rare kinds in P.** Directed kinds as feature positions for T and R; 28 relations on shared bases for P (N 13.2, N 13.8).
- **M6, in part.** Reach of any length by walks and echo, names, words, the reader on the shortlist; a file with none of these is not reached (N 2.3, N 14.4).
- **M7, open on counts 2, 5, 6, 8.** Memory and time argued per step; 24 card-hours accounted; negatives with known π and never right files; weights by D11 (N 13.6; D 3.2).
- **M8, by construction; in part if the share rule fires.** Seven arms from two argued decisions, each neighbour one decision apart, baselines on the same examples, a prediction per arm, per-repository and per-segment reading (D 7.2, D 7.3).

Vetoes nearest: **W9**: b_k has no fitted part; stage 1 offsets out of fold; heads stop on validation; the test is scored once. **W12**: no sweep; the reader's size comes from a formula, the arms from two decisions. **W13**: his two repositories stay on the PC; every download is named; the plan fits 24 card-hours. **W6**: the reader attends within one pair. **W1**: the cosine column outside the shortlist. **W10**: trees read only relative amounts. **W4**: no window; every visible event is counted.

## Cannot
- Aim the reader at what stage 1 misses (A9.b.3): it learns again the partner the bank ranks second.
- Form the conjunction of text and record inside the encoder, only in a head over fixed r (A9.b.3).
- Test C1.r1 with its reader trained through φ, or C3.r1 with adapters inside the encoder; nor run A9.b.3's own-reader test.
- Train P for more than about one epoch; under the share rule P is judged below its data (A10.b.1).
- Read a large interaction except row by row (A10.b.1).
- Remove the in-sample r on training rows; it is only caught by early stopping.
- Everything both designs cannot: a file with no link, word, name or echo; order of events beyond one step; a right file below every shortlist; a loss aimed at the top ten (C1.r1, C3.r1).

## Refuted if
- L does not lead B beyond the intervals on unseen repositories in some tier.
- R or P adds nothing over its stage 1 on right files with no joint history and no two-hop path.
- P leads R overall, or does not lead it on the two-hop-through-matching-text segment.
- T's lead over L is no smaller under P than under R.
- A one-decision contrast changes sign between validation and test beyond the intervals, or an arm leads B only on repositories created before the reader's cut-off.
- The 12 GB form of the chosen arm falls below its rented form beyond the intervals.
