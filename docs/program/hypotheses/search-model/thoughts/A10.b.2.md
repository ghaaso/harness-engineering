# A10 option 2: one cascade, components knocked out
Lens: rival designs side by side; this option departs from it. Each design alone claims most of the eight hours (C1's joint pass about 7 h, C3's reader 5 h), and they share most parts. Two whole designs give one contrast bundling about ten decisions; by N 1.1 the class is the decision, so a bundle names none.
**Mechanism.** Every component of both designs is trained once, in a fixed order, each on the out-of-fold logits of those before it (C3's cascade; D 6.2):
s = b_k + s_T(x) + s_R(h_joint, x) + s_P(h^L) + per-kind terms.
Order: T, then R, then P. Two layers of comparison:
(a) The increment of each stage over the earlier stages, on the same questions.
(b) Knockouts at the head. Encoder, reader and propagation stay frozen. A head over their outputs is refitted with one input removed: marks zeroed, gate neutral, U x̃ removed, hazard H removed, relations merged, raw logs for quantiles, J a constant. These are the "Refuted if" items of C1 and C3, each with its stated segment. Refits take minutes, so each item gets an arm.
Baselines are D12's, on the same examples. Reading as D12: per repository, weighted by 1/π, cluster intervals. Hours: 0–1 trees; 1–4.5 reader; 4.5–7 one P on all three cards; 7–8 head refits, validation, then the test once.
**Needs from other parts.** From C1 and C3: the component definitions and the union of columns. From A7: one loss for every stage on the out-of-fold offset. From A9: stage order and hours, and storage for h_joint and h^L per shortlisted pair.
**Can.** M8 for many single decisions inside the budget. One P is trained, not two, so P gets the data and epochs C1 asked for. It gives the shrinking order directly: a stage whose increment's interval covers zero leaves the 12 GB form.
**Cannot.** Credit depends on order: a later stage gets credit only for what earlier stages missed. Counterexample: if P could learn all T learns, P after T shows nothing, though P alone would match T. A head knockout is not a component trained without that input; the frozen backbone co-adapted with it. It cannot answer "C1 against C3 as wholes", and it never trains a linear stage 1. Nearest veto W12: the knockouts are a list of the designs' own refutations, not a sweep.
**Rests on.** N 1.1, N 9.5, N 13.9, N 14.4, D 6.2, D 7.3, D12; record: FACTS-MODELS-SO-FAR.md (count features alone matched the pair-head network, so T goes first); assumption: out-of-fold offsets keep a stage from fitting its predecessors' training noise.
**Refuted if.** A stage's increment changes sign, beyond intervals, when the stage is retrained on validation without its predecessors: order, not the component, carried the reading.
