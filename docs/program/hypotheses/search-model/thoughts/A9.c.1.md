# A9 option 1: Cross-fitted large reader teaches small one
Lens: large first, then shrink: a large form on the cards teaches a small one for the 12 GB card.

**Mechanism.** In both designs the only large part is the pair reader. The graph part (C1, d ≤ 256) and the trees (C3) are already small. The exact record is computed once, off the cards; C1's x̃ is a subset of C3's R_ex. Chunk and event vectors are made on the PC if the token count allows.
Cards (three; one job each, no sharding; F timed first):
- h0.25–1.25, all: C3's trees on training-cluster halves H1, H2 and on all, then out-of-fold s_0+s_1 and the shortlists K(q).
- h1.25–4.25: teachers T1 on H1 (card 1) and T2 on H2 (card 2), each in C3's s_2 form, a head over [h_joint; R_ex]. Size: 6·N_T·L·P_h = 3 h·F. At 1.6×10^6 pairs of 512 tokens and F = 1.5×10^14, N_T ≈ 3×10^8. Card 3: C1's stage 2 without J.
- h4.25–5.25: each T_i scores the other half's shortlists (out-of-fold targets) and validation and test. The mean of T1 and T2 is the large form.
- h5.25–6.75, cards 1–2: the student, 0.1–0.3 B, same head:
  L = (1−γ)·L_A7 + γτ²·Σ_{c∈K(q)} p_T log(p_T/p_S), with p = softmax over K(q) of (s_0+s_1+s_2)/τ. γ and τ are set on validation.
- h6.75–7.5, all: C1's last epoch with the student as J, tuned through φ. Then scoring.
- h7.5–8: reserve.
Sampling: known probability per repository, equal repository weight (data choice D11); C1's neighbours with known π. Saved: checkpoints every 30 min under a watchdog, every arm's scores per (question, candidate), targets, weights. 12 GB form: the student plus the small parts, with record and trees on the processor. It reads the owner's two repositories, which never leave his PC (D15).

**Needs from other parts.** From C3: the halves, R_ex, the shortlist rule, an hour of trees. From C1: about 3 card-hours per stage-2 epoch, the largest graph resident, J as a pooled vector. From A10: no arms that retrain the reader. From A7: a loss over K(q).

**Can.** M7, memory, by construction: a 2×10^9 reader with optimiser state takes about 32 GB of 96. Time is open on counts. The soft targets carry the teacher's order among wrong files, which matters where labels are few (task words, errors). M8: both designs and the baselines are scored on the same saved examples. M2 and M4 are kept.

**Cannot.** C1 loses its end-to-end J: J is only tuned for one epoch. Each teacher saw half the data. There is no room for reader arms. Shared teacher errors pass to the student. At N_T ≈ 3×10^8 the large form fits 12 GB itself, so the student buys time per question and shortlist width, not memory. Nearest veto W9: in-fold targets would pass on memorised labels. Cross-fitting avoids that.

**Rests on.** N 1.1, N 8.4, N 13.6, N 14.1, N 14.2; D 3.2, D 6.2, D 6.4; record: FACTS-MODELS-SO-FAR.md. Assumption: soft listwise targets inform beyond one to three right files. Count first: F, pairs, largest graph.

**Refuted if.** The mean teacher does not lead the student on validation repositories. Or a student of equal size trained on labels alone matches the distilled one within the intervals.
