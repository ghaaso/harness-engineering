# A9 option 1: frozen stages, cached outputs, out-of-fold cascade
Lens: staged: compute and store first what is exact or frozen, then train the learned part.

**Mechanism.** Four stages, each written before the next.
- S0, processors, before renting: one time-ordered pass per repository writes the exact record per kept (question, candidate): R(q) whole plus one Poisson draw of the rest, π_c stored. One table holds C3's R_ex, C1's x̃, b_k and the baselines: every arm sees the same rows.
- S1, frozen encoder, forward only (2N operations per token): chunk, event and question vectors; on the PC if the count fits a day.
- S2: cheap learned stages, out of fold by halves of training clusters: C3's trees, C1's linear stage 1. Shortlists written.
- S3: readers trained against frozen offsets from stored token packs. C1's joint reader trains on L2 alone; its J(q,v) is cached; C1's propagation trains on cached J.

Cards A, B, C; effective rate assumed 1.5×10^14 until measured.
- h0–0.5: confirm 96 GB; measure the rate and C1's step on the largest repository; sizes by N = C/(6LP).
- h0.5–1.5: C3 trees.
- h1.5–6.5, A: C3 reader (at 7.5×10^6 pairs, N ≈ 1.2×10^8); h6.5–7 teacher scores dumped.
- h1.5–5, B: C1 joint reader; h5–5.5 J cached.
- h1.5–5.5, C: C1 propagation, J flagged absent; h5.5–7, B and C: with J.
- h7–8: validation, settings, test once.
Card-hours: C3 8.5, C1 11, shared 4.5. Saved per stage and half hour; a watchdog per job. Memory: a 1.8×10^9 reader with optimiser under 40 GB; vectors in host memory.
12 GB form: S0 and S2 on processors; the reader in half precision if under 8 GB, else distilled on the PC from the teacher scores.

**Needs from other parts.** C3 unchanged: trees, s_2 on out-of-fold offsets, its sizing rule. C1: x̃, stage 1, φ, J with its absence flag, propagation; changed: J's adapters no longer train through propagation. A7: the draw and π. A10: whether card-hours must be equal per design.

**Can.** M2 by construction: exact counts over all history, never sampled. M4 by construction: one cut per asking time, tested on the stored table; out-of-fold offsets keep stage-1 overconfidence out of stage 2. M7 open on counts.

**Cannot.** Train C1's stages for each other: a content cue useful only beside a history pattern is dropped by J trained alone. Average over negative draws: a hard negative not drawn is never seen. Upload cheaply: about 275 GB at 2.5×10^8 rows. Nearest veto W9: P excluded, settings on validation, offsets out of fold.

**Rests on.** N 14.4, N 14.2; D 2.10, D 3.2, D 6.4, D 9.4, D 13; record: FACTS-MODELS-SO-FAR.md; assumption: an in-fold offset is too good on its own questions; count first: record bytes, tokens, PC encoder rate, edges per repository.

**Refuted if.** End-to-end C1 (option 2) beats staged C1 beyond the intervals where content and history combine; a second negative draw moves per-repository ranks beyond the intervals; stage times exceed the plan by half.
