# A10 option 1: two-by-two of stage one and stage two
Lens: rival designs side by side.
**Mechanism.** C1 and C3 share a spine: candidates, b_k, exact columns, A7.c.1's loss and negatives, a joint reader of question and content. Reader size is a setting, so one reader is trained and both use it. The designs differ in two decisions: stage 1 linear (L, C1) or trees (T, C3); stage 2 a head over [h_joint; x] (R, C3) or typed gated propagation with marks (P, C1). Arms (same questions, same candidates): B (D12's baselines and b_k), L, T, L+R, T+R (= C3), T+P, L+P (= C1). Neighbours differ in one decision.
Hours on three cards: 0–1 L and T fits (out of fold), leftover encoder vectors. 1–4 the reader, N = C/(6LP) with C = 3 cards × 3 h; h_joint stored per shortlisted pair. 4–7 T+P on card a, L+P on card b; card c fits R heads and scores validation. 7–8 settings chosen on validation; the test scored once for every arm.
Reading: per repository, weighted by 1/π, intervals by cluster resampling inside strata (D12). A contrast counts if its interval on unseen repositories after the cut-off excludes zero. Predictions, each on a segment named now:
- L vs B: L ahead in every tier.
- T vs L: gain where two columns must combine (bulk-flagged repositories; trace depth with counts).
- R vs stage 1: gain on right files with no joint change and no two-hop path, most for task words; near zero where joint history exists.
- P vs R: equal overall; P ahead only where a two-hop path runs through a middle file or event whose text matches the question.
- Interaction: T's lead over L shrinks under P, since U x̃ lets P form conjunctions.
The smallest arm whose interval overlaps the best arm's goes to the 12 GB form.
**Needs from other parts.** From C1: x, b_k, P with marks, gate, U x̃, φ. From C3: R_ex, trees with per-kind residuals, the R head, the shortlist. x is the union of both. From A9: tokens, questions per kind, one P epoch's time on one card.
**Can.** M8 by construction. Tests both designs' central claims on one set of examples. M7 open on A9's counts.
**Cannot.** Test C1's reader at C1's size. The reader trains on T's residual, so L arms read vectors aimed at T's misses. If a P epoch on one card exceeds three hours, both P arms train on one drawn share of repositories, and C1 is judged below its data. A large interaction confines reading to rows. Nearest veto W12: no sweep; seven arms follow from two argued decisions.
**Rests on.** N 1.1, N 9.4, N 13.9, D 7.2, D 7.3, D 9.2, D12; record: FACTS-MODELS-SO-FAR.md (the pair-head network added nothing over its count features); count first: tokens, P's epoch time.
**Refuted if.** One-decision contrasts change sign between validation and test beyond intervals, or an arm leads B only on repositories created before the encoder's cut-off.
