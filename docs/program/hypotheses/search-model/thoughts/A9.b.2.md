# A9 option 2: end to end, recomputed on the card
Lens: staged: compute and store first what is exact or frozen, then train the learned part. Departs: nothing per pair is stored; C1's parts train together.

**Mechanism.** Per repository the card holds a time-sorted incidence matrix B (events by main-line arrival, files) and the link graph with each edge's birth time. The view at t is the prefix B[:, :n(t)]. A batch holds questions of one repository. Inside the step, the exact columns (bank, hazard, typed paths, walks, echo) are sparse products on the prefix; negatives are a fresh Poisson draw with known π each epoch; C1's stage 1, φ, joint-pass adapters and propagation train on L1 + L2 at once, gradients through the adapters, as C1 wrote it. Frozen encoder vectors stay precomputed (an encoder forward per step would exceed the budget).
C3 is forced off this option: a tree passes no gradient and is fitted on a whole table, so its s_1 and shortlist are staged as in option 1.

Cards A, B, C:
- h0–0.5: confirm 96 GB; time one step on the largest repository.
- A, h0.5–1.5: C3 trees on one card (0.85×10^8 rows); h1.5–6.5: C3 reader.
- B and C, h0.5–6.5, data-parallel: C1 end to end (12 card-hours, 6.5×10^18: at 16 pairs of 512 tokens and 0.15×10^9 parameters, 7×10^12 per question, about two passes over 3×10^5 questions).
- h6.5–8: validation, settings, teacher scores, test once.
Saved half-hourly with the sampler's seed. Memory: B and the graph of the largest repository (count first: edges) plus activations for 16 pairs.
12 GB form: the same loader on processors; readers as in option 1.

**Needs from other parts.** C1 as written (A2.b.1: adapters through φ). C3 unchanged, staged. A7: π per epoch. A5: the prefix as the as-of view, with birth times of links.

**Can.** M4 by construction: the loader computing columns at training is the code used at inference (D 6.4), and nothing dated after t is in the prefix (D 9.4). M2 by construction: recomputed exactly each step. For C1, J learns what propagation lacks. Fresh draws average one draw's variance (N 14.2, D 2.10). M7 open on the step time.

**Cannot.** Serve C3: it gains nothing and loses two thirds of its tree rows to one card. Out-of-fold offsets for C1: stage 1 and the top K move during training, so propagation chases a moving shortlist. Bound a hub repository: its walks over 10^6 edges can dominate an epoch. Run D13's cut-against-whole test on a whole table; only on samples of the loader. Nearest veto W9: P is excluded from each draw.

**Rests on.** N 14.2, N 14.3, N 9.5; D 2.9, D 2.10, D 6.4, D 9.4, D 13; assumption: sparse prefix products run in milliseconds; count first: edges and events per repository, step time.

**Refuted if.** Staged C1 (option 1) matches end-to-end C1 within the intervals where content and history combine; or one step on the largest repository exceeds the plan so that fewer than one pass fits.
