# A9 option 3: Train large, compress the same weights
Lens: large first, then shrink, but by compressing the large form itself, with no student.

**Mechanism.**
1. *All card time goes to the large forms of both designs.* C3's s_2 at N_T solves 6·N_T·L·P = F·h. C1's joint pass is trained at the same N_T through φ, end to end.
2. *The small form is the same function at lower precision.* After training, each reader's weights are rounded to 8 or 4 bits per weight, in blocks with a scale per block. Activations stay in 16 bits. The small form adds nothing learned, only one pass to set the scales on training questions.
3. *12 GB memory:* N_T·b/8 bytes, about 0.2 GB at 3×10^8 and 4 bits. *Time per question:* 2·N_T·L·|K| / F_12, where F_12 is the PC card's low-precision speed, timed.
4. *Cards* (one job per card; F is timed in the first 15 min):
- h0.25–1.25: C3's tree fits and the shortlists.
- h1.25–6.5: card 1 runs C3's s_2 at N_T on all training data. Cards 2–3 run C1 data-parallel with the large J.
- h6.5–7.5: scoring at full and at low precision on validation and test.
- h7.5–8: reserve.
The record is computed once with no card time. Questions are sampled per repository with known probability (data choice D11), and C1's neighbours with known π. Saved: checkpoints every 30 min with a hang watchdog, scores per (question, candidate) at both precisions, and all weights.

**Needs from other parts.** From C1: J trainable at N_T through φ, and the stage-2 cost with a larger J. From C3: the out-of-fold s_0+s_1 for its s_2 offsets. From A10: few reader arms.

**Can.** It keeps C1's end-to-end joint pass, at large size. There is no cross-fitting for targets and no gap between teacher and student. M7 holds by construction for memory, on both card types. It is open on time.

**Cannot.** It cannot cut the operations per question much. If N_T times the shortlist exceeds t_q on the PC card, the shortlist must shrink, which lowers M6. 4-bit rounding may move close scores in the top ten. It leaves no card time for replicates or reader arms (M8 in part). Nearest veto W13: the large pretrained reader is a download that needs the owner's word.

**Rests on.** N 1.1; D 6.4 (the scales are set on training data only). Assumption: rounding keeps the order of scores that are far apart. Count first: F_12 at low precision, and |K|.

**Refuted if.** The low-precision ranks differ from the full-precision ranks beyond the resampling intervals on validation repositories. Or the time per question on the 12 GB card exceeds that of a student of the same quality.
