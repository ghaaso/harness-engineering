Order of work and hand-offs

Each block is one row from the tables above, as wide as its days, and the arrows show where one of us waits on the other. I answer D07 on day 0; the rest of my lane starts once P0a is done. The order holds as long as P0a takes me less than about 12 working days. After that you would reach WP7 before my WP8 contract is ready, so do your rows 10 to 12 (WP5 levers, WP12b, WP6a) before it. Neither of us has fixed weekly hours, so read this as an order, not a calendar.

How we work

  • My FixFlow phases go first on my PC and my usage limit.
  • One token budget per account per five-hour window. Agent runs on my PC are booked in the WP11 queue, and heavy checks take an ff-slot.sh slot.
  • Script before agent, replay before live, one fact one command, state in the repo, and a tag on every number. The 14 November edition explains each.
  • Each of us is the other's fresh reader: you read my WP8 contract and my WP7-finish write-up, and I read your scripts before they may refuse anything.
  • At most two packages in flight each.
  • On your Mac: write scripts in Node so they run on my PC unchanged, and build images for both arm64 and amd64. The Air has no fan, so keep heavy containers short and run one at a time. The graph test and the GNN run stay on my PC, because their locks are x86_64 and CompGCN ran on my GPU.
  • If P0a eats my first weeks, you build WP4's plan check and replay it on your testbed's lane waves, and I run it on FixFlow with my ports. About 1.5 of its 2 days move to you, giving 56 / 44. If more has to move, my lecture pass is the one that suits you, once I copy the lecture library into the repo: 60 / 40 (59.7 %). After that nothing fits under the cap.

What changed since the 14 November edition

  • The compaction summaries are right on facts. They got 0 of 2,727 checkable claims wrong across 117 summaries M. 74 of the 117 lag behind their own hand-over on the latest state, but no next window redid a state-changing step from its kept tail (0 of 117) M.
  • The real problem is after a compaction. Sessions make 1.35 mistakes per 100 calls, against 0.58 before M, mostly forgotten facts about my PC. A brand-new session makes as many (1.13 against 1.14) M, so the split rule does not fix this. The PC-facts card in my WP10a row is aimed at it.
  • The GNN and embeddings run trained its arms and wrote its tuning verdicts. On 16 Nov its held-out run was at 174 of 210 units M.
  • The edition missed two things from 13 Nov: Graphify's held-out score, 0.564 against the typed store's 0.700 M, and my graph v2 design.
  • The harness has its own repository, Tempest-Zero/harness-engineering, since 15 Nov.
  • There are two of us on two machines, and with your own plan we have two five-hour windows. The edition's one-PC rules now apply only to my PC.
  • FixFlow: 13 phases are merged, nothing is deployed and production is still down. My P0a comes first.
