# Release notice (one technician at a time)

**Status: DRAFT — not sent.** Written by the coordinator session on 2026-11-13 for the owner to
edit and send (plan P2b-2 Scope, standing rules 1 and 6). It goes to ONE technician at a time,
and only after that technician's phone has been drained and its after-check has passed (plan
P4b): never to the whole group, and never because "the server is back". When it has gone to a
technician, write "released <date>, <channel>" in that phone's row of the roster table and keep
the text below exactly as sent. Square brackets mark words only the owner can supply; delete a
bracketed sentence that does not apply. The hold notice it ends is `fleet-hold.md`.

## English

The records on your phone have been collected and checked. From now on:

You may:

1. Open the FixFlow app and sign in. [Use the new PIN on the slip you were given.]
2. Clock in and clock out, and start travel, as before.
3. Tap "Retry" on anything the app shows as not sent.

You must still not:

4. Tap "Discard" on anything, unless [name] tells you to for that one item.
5. Uninstall the app, clear its data or storage, or factory-reset the phone.
6. Install the app from any link or file other than the one [name] gives you.
7. Sign in as another person, or let another person work under your name.

If the app shows "Needs attention", a failed item, a message that an item is too old, or anything
you do not understand: do not discard it and do not decide alone. Show the phone to [name] the
same day.

Questions: [name], [phone number].
