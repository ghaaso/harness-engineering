1. **Rail live track diagram.** opentraintimes.com/maps/help and /maps/signalling/bhm. Pictures 13, 12. Screen seen, help page read. A fixed drawing of the real parts (black berths); the mover's four-character id appears inside the part it occupies; the route ahead lights green; signal dots red or green, a hollow white dot = state unknown; `****` in a berth = occupied out of sequence. Goals 1, 2, 5, 9, 12. No lines between movers and parts, so no mesh. Breaks: a berth holds one train; our hook can be hit by 5 sessions at once, so a part needs several slots.

13. **Smooth updating, practice.** bost.ocks.org/mike/constancy and /mike/path (pages read); web.dev/articles/animations-guide (page read); Heer and Robertson 2007 (abstract read: animated transitions "significantly improve graphical perception"). Join data to drawn elements by a stable key so the same thing moves and is never rebuilt; slide a time window with a transform at linear speed, never re-interpolate the path (that is the wiggle); animate only transform and opacity, anything that triggers layout or paint will not stay smooth. Goal 10. Round 7's lag most likely came from redrawing the panel on each read (my inference, not checked).
14. **Change blindness and motion.** en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Change_blindness (page read); Bartram, Ware, Calvert 2001 (abstract read: motion outperforms static cues; some motions are more distracting and irritating). A change made during any interruption (a blank, a flicker, other splashes, looking away) goes unseen even when looked for. So: no full redraw, few things changing at once, and anything that happened while he was away must leave a mark that stays. Small motion in place suits his second screen. Goals 10, 5.

## Three ideas for a prototype, ranked

1. **The fixed parts board with occupancy (rail diagram + HMI colour).** The 60 real parts drawn once, by name, in fixed places grouped by kind (hooks, scripts, skills, commands, helper kinds, tests, git), filling the screen; all grey. A session's short id appears in a slot on the part it is touching now; a failure is a red shape on that part, a refusal a grey ring, not switched on a hollow dot, and it stays until seen. Pictures 13, 08, 03. Goals 1, 2, 5, 9, 11, 12. Reads at a glance because places never move and the only colour is trouble.
2. **Sessions as containers, helpers inside (cluster view + Ableton loop pie).** Each running session is a box holding its helpers as small squares by kind; a loop pie with a count on anything going round; idle sessions shrink to a thin bar. It sits beside or around the board of idea 1, tied to it by shared ids and by highlight on hover or click, never by standing lines. Pictures 15, 09, 10, 04. Goals 2, 4, 9, 12.
3. **Motion rules as part of the design.** Elements keyed and moved, never redrawn; a part pulses once in place when it fires, then fades to an afterglow; traffic lines appear only for the session under the pointer; only transform and opacity animate. Pictures 01, 11, plus finding 13. Goals 10, 12. Reads at a glance because motion means "now" and nothing else.
