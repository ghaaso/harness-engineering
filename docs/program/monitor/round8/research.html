Session tool: what real screens do, and what we take from them

Four researchers, 24 November. Pictures from the products' own pages. Blue line under each = what it means for your tool.

Now

A fixed board of the real parts: every hook, script, skill, command and helper kind by name, in fixed places, all grey. A session shows up on the part it is touching. Trouble is the only colour, and its mark stays until you have seen it.

rail track diagram, control-room screens
History

The week as seven day-strips that are never cut: white where nothing ran, a rising edge where work started, red where something broke. A window onto session lanes with their helpers packed underneath. One thin row per hook on the same time axis.

Nx, Chrome DevTools, Grafana, Sentry
Needs you

One loud mark on the session that is waiting for you, with the answer buttons on it. Sessions in a side rail ordered by need, the waiting ones pushed out of line.

Buildkite, cmux, flight strips
Operate

One prompt box in a fixed place that talks to whatever is selected: nothing selected starts a session, a session selected gets the message. Stop beside Send.

Vibe Kanban, Conductor, Warp
Smooth

Each thing is kept and moved, never redrawn. A part pulses once when it fires, then fades. Only position and transparency animate. Round 7 redrew the whole panel on each read, which is the likely cause of its lag.

D3 object constancy, web.dev, Heer and Robertson 2007
