# Persistent context-retention comparison — 15 October 2026

**The adapter works as an exact checkpoint layer, but this study demonstrated
no improvement over native Codex or maintained notes.** All three arms recalled
every tested fact and completed the actual repair correctly. The adapter used
more reported ordinary input. Its first resumed boundary also exceeded a 4 KiB
aggregate delivery budget, despite each checkpoint envelope fitting individually.

## What was tested

The same synthetic replay fixture, provisional decisions, corrections and
incident evidence were supplied to all arms. N could keep optional notes; M/A
maintained their own initially blank notes under 3 KiB. The controller supplied
no participant answers or replacement notes. Implementation and executable work
were deferred until after the memory probes.

Actual `gpt-6-astra/ultra` persistent sessions used the native default compaction
threshold. Two controller-supplied tool outputs filled history. Exact outgoing
payloads, persisted function outputs and calibrated ordinary-input growth were
checked; first-block growth was approximately 98.6% of the estimate. This avoids
the earlier giant-user-message carryover and dynamic-tool clipping mistakes.
The two probes queried disjoint eight/seven facts with all tools blocked and
observed attempts audited. Native practical tools were enabled afterward.

This is supported native app-server testing. Desktop UI equivalence, broader
coding conversations, maximum useful information capacity and a 2/4/8 KiB
frontier were not established. The adapter restores selected authored state;
it does not semantically compress the complete chat by itself.

## Restoration and byte accounting

A's 2,263-byte authored notes were preserved exactly. Both completed automatic
compactions produced distinct fresh snapshots with complete state, no omissions
or integrity problems, and matching hook context before model continuation.

| A boundary | Selected checkpoint envelope | Additional startup envelope | Combined envelopes |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 1, after resume | 2,894 B | 2,068 B | 4,962 B |
| 2 | 2,894 B | 0 B | 2,894 B |

These are reconstructed canonical hook-envelope sizes; native stdout wire bytes
are not exposed by the supported notifications. Observed context text was
4,686 bytes at boundary 1 and 2,785 bytes at boundary 2. Each selected checkpoint
passed its 4 KiB envelope cap. **The aggregate 4 KiB delivery gate failed at the
first boundary.** Additional resume startup context also contributes to request
input; this is not a claim that the whole remaining chat fits in 4 KiB.

## Preserved interruptions and verifier correction

N hit the account limit during its second remote compaction; A hit it during
its first. Their same persistent sessions resumed after reset. Completed facts,
pressure and earlier probes were not replayed. M completed uninterrupted. All
failed turns and raw errors remain in history and immutable reports.

The first completed A boundary exposed an observer bug: the old verifier selected
startup callback42, overlooking exact checkpoint callback44 before model49.
The corrected matcher requires a unique validated snapshot-linked callback among
all completed same-turn callbacks before model output. It rejects missing,
duplicate, stale, late, corrupt, omitted and oversized restoration evidence.
The original false rejection is retained. A continued directly at probe1;
its completed neutral turn and compaction were not repeated. No A quality result
had been sampled when that correction was declared.

The independent audit therefore reports qualified continuation and exact adapter
delivery as passing, while preserving the unqualified failure flags and marking
the comparison interrupted. Both quota errors and the local verifier correction
are explicit. There is no uninterrupted latency comparison. Active controller
time was 2,152.036 seconds; stopped gaps are excluded, and the original two-hour
elapsed-wall limit was breached.

Treat this as working exact-state restoration with **unproven incremental value**.

does not justify a GPU summarizer, service expansion or a high-context-gain claim.
