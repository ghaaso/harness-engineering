## wordsList
Tags M: measured on this PC, in the owner's repositories or session records. S: an outside study or the price list. G: a guess. A computed number takes the weakest tag of its inputs. Piece of work One fix-sized task of about ten cheap calls. A result is made and checked. Visible or latent Visible: something signals a problem, such as an error or a failing check. Latent: nothing signals that a choice matters. Entry One stored conclusion in the library. Look-alike A stored entry that looks like a match but has a different cause. Gate Whatever decides a result is right: tests, and sometimes a review by Opus. Ladder What happens after a caught failure: one cheap retry, then Opus, then Fable. Promoted check An entry's mechanical check that also runs in the shared tests. It acts at the gate with no lookup. Cost Counted in cheap-call equivalents. One cheap-model call costs one. An Opus call costs the Opus price ratio. Wrong rate The share of pieces of work that do not end accepted and right. Ratio The library's figure divided by the other way's figure. Below one, the library's figure is lower. Middle value The value an unknown starts at, inside its range. Library unknown An unknown that exists only because of the library. Each has a poor end and a good end. Good end The end of a range where the design works as intended. The poor end is the other one. Comes back How much of the work comes back, so an entry could exist for it. A property of the work. Reach Whether an entry gets to the cheap model: written, looked up, found and in the window. Entry quality Whether the entry is right and is used rightly. Running cost What lookups, screens, carrying and writing cost. Corner Comes back, reach and entry quality, each at its poor or its good end. Everything else stays at its middle value. All-good setting Every library unknown at its good end, running cost included. Every other unknown at its middle value. Full fall How far the library's cost and wrong rate fall from the middle values to the all-good setting, averaged. Alone What moving only this gives. Its library unknowns go to their good ends. A share of the full fall. Last What is lost if only this stays. All else is at its good end. A share of the full fall. Tie How far the library unknowns of one group move together across the runs. Rank correlation How steadily a result rises or falls as one unknown rises across the runs. One means always; zero means no steady link. Stock and flow A stock piles up over time and is drawn as a box. A flow fills or drains it, drawn as an arrow with a valve. Loop R or B A chain of causes that returns to its start. R feeds itself. B holds itself back. Seed A whole number that fixes the random draws. The same seed always gives the same runs. Knowledge-store design The owner's earlier design for a store of solved defects in FixFlow. It is not the library mapped here. CI The shared test run on GitHub that checks every change. PR A pull request: a proposed change, reviewed before it joins the main branch. Technical names in the cases Case notes keep some names from the records.
