policy_says.top_first: Opus does the work. If the gate catches a wrong answer, Fable tries. No library.
policy_says.cheap_ladder: The cheap model tries first. A caught failure gets one cheap retry, then Opus, then Fable. No library.
policy_says.library: The same ladder, plus the library. A lookup comes before the cheap attempt. Opus and Fable solves are written back as entries.

leaves_out[4].t: The Opus price ratio is unsure
leaves_out[4].x: The price list gives {{n:2|S}} per token. On this PC an Opus call read about {{n:222,000|M}} cached tokens. A Sonnet call read about {{n:440,000|M}}. So per call the ratio may be nearer one. The runs draw it between {{n:1|G}} and {{n:2|G}}. At one, the cost ratio against the strong model is {{g:ct_last_m1}} instead of {{g:ct_last}}.
leaves_out[5].t: The coverage ceilings lean toward the library

leaves_out[6].t: Chosen cases, not a sample of work
leaves_out[6].x: The {{c:cases}} cases were chosen because something went wrong. They show which routes exist and where steps break. They never show how often.
leaves_out[7].t: Case statuses are judgments
leaves_out[7].x: Works, strains and breaks were set by the agents that walked the cases. Another reader could set some differently. {{c:ks_steps}} of the {{c:steps_total}} step judgments name the earlier knowledge-store design. leaves_out[8].t: One size of work, and no time
leaves_out[8].x: Every piece of work is about ten cheap calls. Real work varies far more in size. Tests at the gate cost nothing. Waiting and the owner's attention are left out.
leaves_out[9].t: Pieces of work are independent
leaves_out[9].x: A piece's kind, its need, what the library holds and any look-alike are combined by multiplying chances. Real work clusters. A hard problem may also be the one the library lacks.
leaves_out[10].t: Averages, not one library
leaves_out[10].x: The stocks change by their expected amounts each period. A real library has one history, with luck in it. Nothing written during a period helps that same period.
leaves_out[11].t: Entries are all-or-nothing
leaves_out[11].x: A good entry is good wherever it applies, and a bad one bad. Partly right shows up only as the 'some parts' result.
leaves_out[12].t: One pool of entries
leaves_out[12].x: Projects are not separated. Facts that differ between projects act only through the look-alike and wrong-entry chances.
leaves_out[13].t: An entry in hand acts only at the cheap attempt
leaves_out[13].x: A wrong entry does not follow the work up the ladder. A good entry does not help the cheap retry or Opus either. Only a promoted check acts later, at the gate.
leaves_out[14].t: A fixed ladder and one gate
leaves_out[14].x: One cheap retry, then Opus, then Fable, in that order. Real sessions skip rungs, repeat them, or end with the owner. The ladder step was 'not reached' in {{c:step8_not_reached}} cases and 'works' in {{c:step8_works}}. In {{c:not_catcher_cases}} cases the higher tier was not the one that caught the error.
leaves_out[15].t: How bad entries come and go
leaves_out[15].x: Staleness moves good entries to bad, never back. A bad entry turns good only when a found escape is repaired. Bad entries leave only at the retiring rate. At the middle values the bad share reaches {{g:bad_share_last}} by the last period.
leaves_out[16].t: Two simplifications favour the library
leaves_out[16].x: The mechanical check accepts every good entry and rejects every wrong one. Repair costs nothing beyond the rework.
leaves_out[17].t: How ranges are drawn
leaves_out[17].x: Drawn on its own, an unknown is spread evenly across its range. Library unknowns can be tied within their group, through that group's shared level. At the middle tie, a library unknown mixes that level with its own draw. Its draws then bunch toward the middle of its range. The middle nine in ten fall between {{n:0.16|G}} and {{n:0.84|G}} of the range. If two chances in a draw add up to more than certainty, both are scaled down. With the current ranges this does not happen. The ranking measures steady rise or fall. It misses an effect that rises and then falls.
leaves_out[18].t: Some limitations the model cannot show
leaves_out[18].x: {{c:noparam}} limitations act through no setting in the model. {{c:partly}} more are shown only in part. Each one says why when opened.
leaves_out[19].t: Some tags are generous
leaves_out[19].x: Several figures tagged M or S were measured on another unit than their setting. Each slider's source says how. One outside figure supports two settings in a row.
